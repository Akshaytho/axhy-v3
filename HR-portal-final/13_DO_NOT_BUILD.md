# 13 — Do Not Build (scope boundaries)

> Source of truth: `99_CANON_FACTS.md` §0, §6, §10; method in `03 §7` (default-deny). The rule: **if it's not traceable to a need in `02` and a surface in the closure spec, it's excluded by default and listed here with its reason.** Scope grows only by adding a cited need.

Three cut-strengths, borrowed from the worker `DO_NOT_BUILD_MVP.md` discipline:

- **HARD CUT** — do not build, do not stub, do not scaffold. Changing this needs founder sign-off.
- **DEFERRED** — real, but a later wave; not now.
- **OUT OF PORTAL** — belongs to another persona's portal (owner / superadmin), not HR.

## HARD CUTS

| #   | Not building                                                                                                            | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| H1  | **Payment / disbursement engine** (money actually moving)                                                               | Payment is manual + salary-only (locked). Payroll-close prepares a file; it does not pay. Building a payment engine now over-promises and risks the wrong-field bug. (`§1`, `05 G1`)                       |
| H2  | **Bulk export / "download all rules or data"**                                                                          | INV 13 — the accumulated data is the moat; no export API anywhere, including audit-chain (which answers questions, not dumps).                                                                             |
| H3  | **Worker/HR self-resign / self-terminate button**                                                                       | LOCKED 2026-05-18 — offboarding always originates from HR/owner; no self-service exit anywhere.                                                                                                            |
| H4  | **"HR proposes → admin approves" gate for supervisor creation**                                                         | LOCKED hiring-hierarchy — HR creates supervisors directly; an approval gate is a forbidden pattern.                                                                                                        |
| H5  | **Worker public self-signup / open registration**                                                                       | LOCKED — new workers are HR-created first; OTP only binds an existing membership.                                                                                                                          |
| H6  | **HR writing company rules (`ai.rules.company.*`) or `ai.limits.*`**                                                    | Rule hierarchy — those are owner-only; HR is confined to Layer 2. Must 403.                                                                                                                                |
| H7  | **Tenant/company creation or an owner/HR-creates-company flow**                                                         | INV 3 — tenant creation is SUPER_ADMIN-only.                                                                                                                                                               |
| H8  | **Hard-delete of any record**                                                                                           | INV 4 — SUPER_ADMIN only; HR anonymizes + soft-deletes.                                                                                                                                                    |
| H9  | **Editing/deleting AuditEvent or Policy rows**                                                                          | INV 8/9 — append-only; the audit chain is the legal record.                                                                                                                                                |
| H10 | **AI "smart pick" that chooses the cover / decides for HR**                                                             | House rule — show 2-3 options, the human decides. The AI surfaces context, never the answer. (`03 §8`)                                                                                                     |
| H11 | **Direct DB status writes** on state-machined entities (Worker)                                                         | INV 10 — transitions only via the machine/endpoint.                                                                                                                                                        |
| H12 | **State-machine jargon in the UI** (`ON_LEAVE`, `DWI`, `EMPLOYMENT-tier`)                                               | Visual-first house style — labels are plain ("On leave").                                                                                                                                                  |
| H13 | **Owner auto-inheriting HR authority when HR is absent**                                                                | LOCKED `closure Decision 2` — the only legal path is the explicit, time-bounded **7-day emergency grant** after the 72h tier; route auth refuses owner-by-default. | H14 | **A membership-update endpoint that accepts bank fields** (`PATCH /admin/memberships/:id` carrying `bankIfsc/bankAcct`) | Bank is **create-only** for HR; _changes_ are an owner surface with 2-step OTP (O1). No HR edit path may re-admit bank — keep the door the bank-change boundary depends on shut.                           |

## Default-deny reminder

Anything proposed that isn't on a build list (`05`, `14`) and isn't traceable to a cited need gets added **here** with a reason, not silently built. If a future request implies a cut feature, surface the cut and ask — don't quietly scaffold it. (`03 §7`; `DO_NOT_BUILD_MVP.md:4`)
