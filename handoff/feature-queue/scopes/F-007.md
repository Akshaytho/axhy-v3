## 1. Purpose

When HR creates a binding (acting cover, permanent rebind, or `BINDING_ENDED_AUTO` fires after `effectiveUntil`), F-004 + F-003 emit AuditEvent rows but no notification reaches the affected workers or the involved supervisors. F-007 closes that persistence gap:

1. Subscribe to `HANDOFF_PACKAGE_GENERATED` (F-004) and `BINDING_ENDED_AUTO` (F-003) via outbox topic `notification.supervisor_change`.
2. Resolve audience point-in-time at the event's `effectiveAt`: workers (`Assignment.state='ACTIVE'` on `siteId`) + outgoing supervisor (may be null) + incoming supervisor (never null).
3. Compose plain-language `supervisor_change` notifications, localised to recipient's `preferredLanguage` (worker) / `locale` (supervisor).
4. Write Notification rows: for each (recipient × channel × site), one immutable row with `deliveredAt=NULL`.
5. **No audit emit from F-007 round 1.** `WORKER_SUPERVISOR_CHANGE_NOTIFIED` fires at delivery time in F-011, not at persistence time in F-007 (audit-kind semantics require actual delivery to the worker).
6. **No coalescing in persistence.** Workers + supervisors both one-per-site. Multi-site bursts produce N source events → N row sets. Grouping is presentation-side (F-006 / F-007b).
