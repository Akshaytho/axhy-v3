# AXHY HR Payroll — Complete Build Spec (research-backed, no second-guessing)

**Date:** 2026-06-06 · **Source:** 10-dimension deep research (facility/staffing model, full lifecycle, India compliance/Payment-of-Wages/CLRA/Code-on-Wages, disputes, fraud/money-safety, attendance integration, non-expert-HR, corrections/reporting, worker mobile, novel UX) + founder confirm + the HR Payroll v2 UI mock (docs/design/payroll-ui-v2/).

## Vision

AXHY Payroll is the world's most fearless-to-run payroll: a non-expert HR at a small Indian cleaning company closes the month in under 10 minutes by reviewing only what's wrong, every worker sees exactly why every rupee moved, and nobody — worker or company — can ever be cheated, because every number carries its proof.

## Pay model — THE answer

DECISIVE: Pay is WORKER-BASED monthly salary, NOT site-based. The single source of truth is `Membership.baseSalaryPaise` — one monthly salary attached to the worker's employment with the company, independent of how many sites (0, 1, or many) they are assigned to in a given month. The rule everything rests on: SALARY IS OWED FOR EMPLOYMENT, NOT FOR WORK-AVAILABILITY. A Site is purely an assignment/attendance venue (`Assignment(workerId, siteId, validFrom, validUntil)`); it gates WHERE attendance is captured and therefore WHICH days can count as worker-caused absence — it never gates whether salary is owed. The only legitimate reasons salary is reduced are (a) pro-ration for partial employment within the month (joined/exited mid-month), and (b) LOP for WORKER-CAUSED absence (unauthorized/leave-exhausted/disciplinary). Lack-of-assignment, lack-of-task, site-contract loss, and inter-site transfer NEVER reduce salary — those are the company's revenue risk, never the worker's pay risk. This is both the correct India-law position (Industrial Disputes Act + Code on Wages: employer-created work stoppage = full pay) and the thing that makes AXHY trustworthy: the #1 wage-theft failure in competitor systems is auto-LOP'ing a benched worker, and AXHY structurally cannot do it because LOP is sourced ONLY from explicit absence records, never from "no active site."

## Site relationship (expiry / headcount-cut / bench / transfer / multi-site)

Site is the attendance/assignment venue, never the payer. Each site case resolves on the payslip as follows. (1) SITE-EXPIRY (contract ends mid-month, e.g. 20th): worker continues full monthly salary; if employment also ends, pro-rate (salary ÷ calendar-days × days-employed) and trigger terminal settlement within 2 working days — but if worker stays employed and is benched/reassigned, NO reduction at all. Payslip line: "Assignment [Site] ended 20th — salary unaffected (worker-based pay)." (2) HEADCOUNT-CUT: this is a company decision to retain or release the worker; if retained → paid bench, full salary; if released → retrenchment/exit settlement, not LOP. Never auto-LOP. (3) BENCH: DEFAULT = PAID bench — assigned-to-company-but-no-site = full monthly salary, zero LOP. Unpaid bench is OFF by default and only legal if (a) company config explicitly enables it AND (b) the worker refused offered work (logged reason). Payslip: "Benched (no assignment) — Paid in full. Company policy: Paid Bench." (4) TRANSFER (site→site mid-month): zero salary impact regardless of transfer date; one Assignment closes (validUntil = transfer date), next opens (validFrom = same date) with no gap. Attendance source switches; salary unchanged. Payslip shows the timeline, not a deduction. (5) MULTI-SITE (concurrent, e.g. morning Site-A + evening Site-B): ONE monthly salary, never split, never doubled. Attendance tracked per-site for compliance; an absent DAY is counted once across all sites (absent from either = 1 absent day, not 0.5+0.5 unless half-day rules apply); LOP applied once per month. Payslip shows per-site attendance breakdown but a single unified salary. (6) NO-ACTIVE-SITE for whole month: paid bench, full salary, no LOP. The payslip's golden rule: every site event renders as an informational annotation on the attendance/assignment timeline, and ONLY worker-caused absence renders as a deductible LOP line.

## India compliance must-haves

- PAY-BY DATE (hard): monthly wages within 7th of next month (Code on Wages §17); exit Full&Final within 2 WORKING DAYS; gratuity within 30 days. System warns approaching, blocks/flags overdue, shows penalty exposure (₹7,500+/instance + 12-18% interest).
- 50% DEDUCTION CAP: total deductions ≤ 50% of gross in any wage period; fines separately ≤ 3% of monthly wages; net pay can NEVER drop below applicable state minimum wage — hard-block at approval.
- BASIC ≥ 50% RULE (Code on Wages 2019): Basic+DA must be ≥ 50% of gross; salary-structure save blocked if violated; PF/gratuity/leave-encashment computed on this base.
- MANDATORY PAYSLIP FIELDS (Code on Wages §50): company name/address; worker name/ID/PAN/UAN/ESIC-IP; pay period + date; each earning itemized (Basic, DA, HRA, each allowance, OT); each deduction itemized with calculation basis (PF 12% of basic, ESI 0.75%, PT, TDS, LOP, advance, fine); gross; total deductions; net; employer contributions (EPF, ESI, gratuity provision) shown for transparency; clearly label wages (Basic+DA) vs excluded (HRA/bonus). NO lump 'Other Deductions'. Issue within 5 days of run.
- STATUTORY REGISTERS auto-generated: Wage Register, Muster Roll (Form XVI), Wage Register (Form XVII), Employment Card (Form XIV), Fine Register (with show-cause + 90-day window), Overtime Register. Inspector-ready PDF/Excel export. 3-year retention minimum (forever in AXHY).
- LOP FORMULA fixed: baseSalary ÷ CALENDAR-days-in-month × absent-days (Code on Wages convention); applies ONLY to worker-caused absence; never auto-applied for lack-of-assignment; never reduces net below minimum wage.
- DATA MODEL ROOM for statutory deductions: PF (12% emp + 12% employer, capped ₹15k basic, only ≥20 employees), ESI (0.75% emp + 3.25% employer on gross ≤ ₹21k, ≥10 employees), PT (state-specific slabs), TDS (Income Tax Act, Form 130 annual). Each as a typed PayslipLine with calculation basis + employer-contribution shadow lines. Frozen policy snapshot per PayRun.
- GRATUITY: 1-year milestone (Social Security Code 2020, eff. Nov 2025); (15/26) × eligible-salary × years; tax-exempt to ₹20L; 30-day payment.
- BONUS (Payment of Bonus Act): eligible if wage ≤ ₹21k & worked 30+ days/yr; 8.33%–20% of Basic+DA capped at ₹7,000/mo salary ceiling; annual.
- PRINCIPAL EMPLOYER LIABILITY (CLRA §21): track whether each payment is direct (AXHY paid) or delegated; if delegated and contractor/client defaults, AXHY is guarantor — non-waivable; flag and pay directly.
- MINIMUM WAGE FLOOR per state+area(A/B/C)+occupation(cleaning notified): central cleaning rates Apr-2026 Area A ₹827 / B ₹693 / C ₹556 per day; apply HIGHER of central/state; effective-date tracking + retroactive update; block any payslip where pro-rata gross < applicable minimum.
- SHOW-CAUSE before fine; written 30-day notice before policy changes; worker entitled to itemized payslip + own attendance + statutory deduction breakdown (Code on Wages transparency).

## Dispute & trust design

Make pay UNDISPUTABLE by pairing every number with its proof and giving the worker a one-tap, SLA-backed channel. DESIGN: (1) EVERY payslip line carries WHAT/HOW-MUCH/HOW(formula or policy §+date)/WHY(statute/policy citation). Tapping a LOP line opens the attendance calendar showing the exact absent dates and the system's inference reason ('No attendance 15-18 Jun; Attendance Policy v2.1 §3.2'). No line is ever a bare 'Other Deduction ₹X'. (2) ONE-TAP DISPUTE on any line → form (which line / what happened / upload photo/witness / preferred contact: WhatsApp/phone/app) → generates ref# with 48h HR-response SLA shown in-thread. HR reply cites attendance + policy + recalculation; if worker still disagrees, visible escalation to GRC (mandatory for 20+ workers, equal employer/worker members, 30-day decision) and then SAMADHAN/labour-commissioner (auto-pre-filled with payslip + dispute details) if unresolved in 7 days or dispute > ₹2,000. (3) IMMUTABLE PROOF BOTH WAYS: every PAID payslip is frozen and re-downloadable forever; every correction is a NEW linked payslip ('CORRECTED', predecessor marked 'SUPERSEDED'), never an in-place edit; every edit logs user/timestamp/before/after/reason — searchable by worker, field, month, reason. Worker can download their own audit log. (4) DELIVERY PROOF: payslip pushed via WhatsApp (primary) on a consistent day/time with summary + encrypted link; delivery/open tracked; if not opened in 7 days, auto-escalate to HR. (5) PLAIN LANGUAGE + VOICE in worker's language so a 3rd-standard-literacy worker never has to ask 'why is my money short'. The trust equation: transparent-by-default for the worker (sees everything, before payday too) + immutable-by-default for the company (frozen records + reason-tracked overrides defend to worker, auditor, and labour inspector). This is the exact pairing competitors miss — they show numbers without context (worker assumes theft) and lock records without a fix path (HR can't correct fairly).

## Fraud & money safety

LIGHT-BUT-REAL maker-checker (not bureaucratic): payroll is prepared by a Maker role and the mark-paid 'commit' requires an Owner/Checker digital sign-off — no single person runs DRAFT→PAID end-to-end. GHOST-WORKER defense: monthly attendance-vs-payroll reconciliation flags any worker with zero biometric/GPS punches in 30+ days but an active salary line; Aadhaar-match + quarterly in-person supervisor re-verification required; duplicate Aadhaar/PAN detection. DOUBLE-PAY prevention: hard dedup on (workerId, month, year) — a second PAID payslip for the same worker/month is blocked unless explicitly tagged off-cycle (bonus/arrears) with reason; UTR/reference is MANDATORY on every mark-paid (no blank); pre-pay dedup on (worker + account + amount + date within 1-min window). WRONG-ACCOUNT defense: bank account / IFSC / account-holder name are OWNER-ONLY editable; any change requires owner approval + worker re-verification (SMS/call), and every edit attempt (even failed) is logged; salary diverted to a changed account is the #1 collusion vector, so account-change events are surfaced on the owner dashboard. SKIMMING defense: the person who marks-paid cannot be the person who reconciles the bank statement; AI anomaly copilot flags coordinated approvals (same two users >10×/month), salary spikes >15% month-on-month (require reason, don't block), round-number cash advances, new-account-same-day-multiple-workers, post-exit disbursals, off-cycle pays. RECONCILIATION: monthly bank-statement-vs-payroll match by UTR (₹5 rounding tolerance); mismatches escalate same-day; systematic UTR capture cuts exceptions 40-60%. PII ACCESS (DPDP 2025): role-based — Owner sees salary+bank+Aadhaar; Payroll Manager sees salary; HR sees name+status only; AES-256 at rest+in-transit; every PII access logged + monthly owner audit; worker can request 'who viewed my data'. LOP can NEVER be sourced from a payroll-admin override — it is computed only from the attendance register (which the admin cannot mutate), closing the false-LOP-skim loophole. Cash disbursement (exceptional): face-blurred receipt photo + worker & supervisor co-signature, same-day reconciliation. Architecture principle: it must be structurally impossible for one actor to both approve a payment and hide it from independent bank reconciliation.

## Attendance integration

CUT-OFF: a hard monthly cut-off (default 25th, config) locks attendance into the run; a 1-2 day automated-reminder grace precedes it, then escalation path for genuine late entries — but no silent edits after lock. PROVISIONAL→FINAL: before cut-off, attendance is PROVISIONAL and VISIBLE to worker + supervisor with a dispute window; supervisor must explicitly sign-off ('Attendance reviewed & approved') to transition provisional→final; after final it is immutable into the run. FOUR-STATE mapping (no orphaned days): every calendar day is exactly one of Present / On-Duty / Leave(paid) / LOP(unpaid); holidays + weekly-offs auto-marked paid and LOP-exempt. SOURCE OF TRUTH: LOP is computed ONLY from the attendance register (biometric/GPS/visit-based + supervisor muster), never from a payroll-admin field — admins cannot mutate attendance, only flag exceptions for review. VISIT-BASED (cleaning): 1+ visits/day = Present (default), 0 visits = Absent unless pre-approved leave, partial/half-visit = supervisor override with GPS timestamp + reason (never auto-LOP a delayed/locked-site visit). CORRECTION-AFTER-RUN: pre-approval (DRAFT) → re-enter with reason and re-run; post-PAID → off-cycle correction only (CFO+HR sign, reason logged, reversing line, never in-place). DRILL-DOWN: the worker (and HR) taps any LOP/Leave/Present line on the payslip → attendance calendar with marked days, selfie/GPS proof, inferred-absence reason, and the policy §+date that produced the deduction; multi-site shows per-site attendance blocks with absence counted once. RECONCILIATION: monthly attendance-system-vs-payslip paid-days reconciliation (target <5% adjustments, flag supervisors with >10% manual-edit rate as fraud signal). ASSIGNMENT GATING: Assignment(validFrom/Until) gates which days can count as LOP — days outside an active assignment are excluded by default (benched = paid), so attendance gaps from transfers/exits never produce phantom LOP.

## site-staffing-model

AXHY's pay model is DEFINITIVELY WORKER-BASED monthly salary, NOT site-based. Worker has a single monthly salary (baseSalaryPaise) on a Membership; sites are purely assignment/attendance locations. Salary is payable regardless of site, and when a site-contract ends mid-month, workers continue earning (pro-rated for that month), with immediate settlement within 2 working days under Code on Wages, 2019. Benched workers (assigned but no active work) MUST be paid their full monthly salary under Indian Industrial Disputes Act and Wages Code — employers cannot withhold pay for work stoppages created by employer circumstance. Multi-site workers get ONE monthly salary (not allocated per-site); LOP applies only for worker absence, never for lack-of-assignment. On contract/site loss, NO auto-LOP — terminal settlement triggers (unpaid days salary + gratuity pro-rata if 1+ year service). This matches Quess/BVG/ISS model: facility labour is contractor/company relationship, not site-based purchase. Principal Employer liable for wage guarantees if site-client defaults.

Attendance tracked per-site for compliance/LOP-avoidance but salary is unified (monthly Membership rate).; MID-MONTH TRANSFER (SITE-TO-SITE): No salary impact; worker keeps monthly salary regardless of transfer date. Only reason for pro-ration: start-of-month or end-of-month contract change.; WAGE PAYMENT TIMELINE: Wages due by 7th of next month (if <1000 workers) or 10th (if ≥1000 workers). On exit, pay within 2 working days (all earned wages, no withhold except TDS/tax).; LOP CALCULATION FORMULA (when valid): LOP = (monthly-gross-salary ÷ calendar-days) × absent-days. Applies only to worker absence (unauthorized, leave-exhausted, or disciplinary). Never auto-applied for lack-of-assignment.

**Edge cases:**

- **Site-contract ends 15th of month; worker employed since Jan 1 this year** → default: Pay pro-rata for days 1–15: (salary ÷ 30) × 15. Gratuity pro-rata: (15/26) × eligible-salary × (15 months of service ÷ 12). Terminal settlement by day 2 of exit. · override: HR can negotiate extended payment terms with worker (rare); cannot reduce pro-rata amount without worker consent. · ui: Show calculation breakdown: 'Salary earned: ₹[amount] for 15 days' + 'Gratuity payable: ₹[pro-rata amount] (1 yr+ milestone triggered)' + 'Pay deadline: [2 days from exit]'.
- **Worker assigned to site, shows up daily, but site-client has no work tasks for 10 days (not worker's fault)** → default: Full salary paid for month — zero LOP. Worker is on paid bench because assignment is active; lack of task ≠ lack of assignment. Site-client or employer must manage workflow. · override: Only if contract says 'paid bench only if assigned-to-site but work availability <X hours/day' — then hourly calculation applies (not common for facility labour). · ui: Payslip shows: 'Assigned to [Site]: [Start–End dates] | Bench days: 10 | Paid: Yes (Company policy: paid bench for active assignment)'. No LOP line.
- **Worker unwell, no doctor's note; absent 5 days in month. Then assigned to new site for last 5 days of month.** → default: LOP for 5 absent days: (salary ÷ 30) × 5, deducted from salary. - **Company loses site-contract 20th of month; 10 workers affected mid-month** → default: Pro-rate all 10 workers for 20 days work. If 1+ year service, gratuity pro-rata on each. - **Worker benched for entire month (no assignment offered, no contract loss — just no work available)** → default: PAID BENCH — full monthly salary payable. Worker is still employed; no LOP. Reflects that labour is hired month-to-month regardless of assignment availability (Indian contract-labour standard). · override: HR can negotiate 'zero-bench' clause (worker must be assigned or laid off), but default is paid bench. - **Fixed-term contract worker employed for exactly 1 year; contract ends on anniversary** → default: Full month's salary paid. Terminal settlement within 2 days. · override: None — statutory once 1+ year mark is crossed (new Social Security Code, 2020, effective Nov 2025). · ui: Alert: 'Gratuity Milestone: 1 year service completed. ## Mid-Month Employee Lifecycle Events in Indian Payroll (JOIN/EXIT/TRANSFER/PROMOTION/SUSPENSION/SALARY-REVISION/TERMINATION)

Comprehensive defaults + HR override catalogue for AXHY's blue-collar payroll system covering 8 lifecycle events (join, exit/termination with full-and-final, transfer, promotion, suspension/hold, mid-month salary revision, advance recovery, retro corrections). Based on Labour Code 2026 (effective April 1, 2026), Payment of Wages Act 2019, Indian state variations, and field-tested practices from Keka/Zoho Payroll/greytHR. Every scenario has a non-negotiable default (pro-rata divisor = 26 working days + calendar-day overrides, 2-day wage settlement mandate, PF/ESI ceiling recalculations) and an editable HR override for company policy variance. The system treats salary as worker-based monthly fixed amount; site is attendance/assignment context only. Arrears, carry-forward advances, LOP reversal, and retro payment reconciliation are retro-capable.

**Rules:** MID-MONTH JOIN (Pro-Rata): Divisor = 26 working days (India legal standard per payroll industry practice); formula = Monthly Salary ÷ 26 × Days Worked. Override: HR can switch to calendar-day divisor (÷30) if contractually stated. Applied to: Basic + DA + Retaining Allowance only; exclude performance incentives (non-wage per Labour Code 2026). EPF pro-rata uses same working-day divisor; ESI calculated on pro-rata gross. Default: Zero gratuity eligibility until 1 year continuous service (fixed-term workers eligible after 1 yr per Labour Code 2026 amendment).; MID-MONTH EXIT / TERMINATION: 2-Day Wage Payment Rule (Code on Wages 2019): Unpaid salary, leave encashment, bonuses, notice-period adjustments MUST pay within 2 working days. Gratuity = separate 30-day timeline. Pro-rata salary = Salary ÷ 26 × Days Worked (same join divisor). Full-and-Final Settlement comprises: [Credits] unpaid salary, earned leave encashment (× daily basic salary), notice-period pay if not served, gratuity if eligible (5+ yrs continuous), other reimbursements → [Deductions] unserved notice recovery (Salary ÷ 26 × shortfall days), outstanding advances, TDS, professional tax, asset recovery. Override: State-specific Shops & Establishment Act may override leave encashment caps; HR audits for state compliance.; GRATUITY CALCULATION: (Last-drawn basic salary × 15 × years of service) ÷ 26 = gratuity paise. Eligibility: 5+ years continuous service (or 1 year for fixed-term workers per Labour Code 2026 as of 21 Nov 2025). Tax-exempt ceiling: ₹20,00,000 for private sector. Payment within 30 days of exit. Override: HR can mark ineligible if contractually excluded (e.g., contract labour, agency workers are employer/contractor responsibility).; NOTICE PERIOD RECOVERY: Legal notice = state-specific (typically 30 days for blue-collar). If employee quits without serving full notice, deduct unserved days as: Salary ÷ 26 × unserved days. Default: full deduction unless HR waives for rehire risk/retention. Cannot exceed total FnF settlement. Override: HR overrides to zero recovery for specific terminations (layoff, retrenchment).; ADVANCE/LOAN RECOVERY: Monthly deduction = advance balance ÷ max installments (default: 11 months, can extend per agreement). Deduction cap: 50–80% of net monthly pay to avoid wage loss spiral. Carry-forward balance: track outstanding balance across months; cannot issue new advance until prior 6-month balance cleared. On exit: full outstanding balance deducted from FnF settlement. Override: HR can modify installment count or increase deduction % with employee written consent; can waive balance in lieu of severance.; APPROVED LEAVE vs UNAPPROVED ABSENCE vs PENDING LEAVE: [Approved Paid Leave] = No LOP, full salary (holidays/weekly-offs excluded from leave count per WageIndicator rule). [Unapproved Absence] = LOP deduction applies: Salary ÷ Working Days in Month × Absent Days. [Pending Leave] = Marked in HRMS but approval not finalized by payroll cutoff → default to LOP until approved retro. LOP Reversal: if leave approved after LOP already deducted, reverse LOP in next month as positive adjustment. Working days divisor = 22 days (default India calculation, excludes 8 Sundays + 1–2 public holidays). Override: HR switches to calendar-day divisor (÷30) if company policy mandates; must be documented.; LOSS OF PAY (LOP) CALCULATION: LOP = (Monthly Salary ÷ Working Days) × LOP Days. Working Days default = 22 (typical India month: 30 calendar days − 8 Sundays − ~1–2 public holidays). Override: use 26-day divisor if company standard; use 30-day divisor if contractually stated. LOP reduces: basic salary (affects EPF), ESI gross (may drop below ₹21k threshold), professional tax slab, and gratuity baseline. Unapproved absence triggers LOP; approved leave does NOT. State variation: each state's Shops & Establishment Act may define differently; HR inputs state code for auto-compliance.; HALF-DAY ATTENDANCE: = 0.5 working day in divisor. If employee works 21.5 days in 22-day month: LOP = (Salary ÷ 22) × 0.5. No deduction from wages for company-closed half-days (Factories Act). Override: HR can log half-day as full day (edge case: employee came sick but worked) or zero day (employee absent without notice, no illness approval).; WEEKLY OFF & PUBLIC HOLIDAY EXCLUSION: Weekly off (7th day mandatory per law) and national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti) do NOT count as working days in divisor. If employee takes 30-day leave including 4 weekly offs + 1 holiday, leave charged = 25 days only. Public holiday work = employee entitled to 2× pay + compensatory leave within 90 days OR 1× pay + 1× daily wage (state/company policy choice). Default: 2× pay option to maximize fairness. Override: HR applies 1× pay option if company budget constraint (with employee consent). Divisor = 22 remains constant regardless of holidays in that month.; MID-MONTH SALARY REVISION: New salary = effective from [Revision Date] in same month. Arrears calculation = (New Salary − Old Salary) ÷ 26 × Days from Revision Date to Month-End. Example: Salary revised to ₹10,000 from ₹8,000 on 15th of 26-day month = (2,000 ÷ 26) × 12 = ₹923 arrears in current month. Old salary applies to days 1–14; new salary + arrears to days 15–26. Which salary applies = Revision Effective Date governs. Recalculate: PF (if arrears push over ₹15k ceiling, only ₹15k PF charged; excess is non-wage), ESI (if over ₹21k, exempt), PT (state-dependent), TDS (use current-month slab; Form 10E relief available if spans fiscal year). Override: HR can defer revision to next month-start to simplify; can recalculate as full month at new rate if employee promotion (policy choice).; PROMOTION / SUPERVISOR UPGRADE: Salary change effective date = Promotion Date. If mid-month: split salary as [Old Salary for days 1–N] + [New Salary for days N+1–EOM] + arrears. Example: Promoted 16th from Worker (₹8k) to Supervisor (₹12k) in 26-day month = (₹8k ÷ 26 × 15) + (₹12k ÷ 26 × 11) + arrears on diff. New allowances/role pay apply from promotion date. Recalculate PF/ESI/PT month-on-month. Override: HR can apply new salary retroactively to month-start for simplification (must document in promotion memo). Gratuity baseline = highest salary drawn in final 12 months (includes promoted salary).; TRANSFER (Worker → Different Site): Salary DOES NOT CHANGE. Assignment record updates (Assignment.validUntil = Transfer Date, new Assignment.validFrom = same date). Attendance tracking switches sites. No payroll impact UNLESS site has site-specific allowances (e.g., remote location bonus). If site allowance present, treat as salary revision: arrears apply for days at old site vs new site. Default: no site-based pay, so zero arrears. Override: HR inputs site allowance delta for arrears calculation.; SUSPENSION / SALARY HOLD: Legal rule: Cannot arbitrarily withhold full salary. Options: (1) Pay Suspension Allowance (typically 50% of gross or state-defined minimum), (2) Full LOP if suspension reason is unpaid leave approval. Suspension Allowance = documented in company policy, must ≥ state minimum wage. PF/ESI contributions continue on suspension allowance base. Default: 50% of basic salary as suspension allowance (must pre-define in Policy table). Override: HR enters custom suspension allowance % per suspension event; can recalculate daily if suspension lifted mid-month. Absconding (3+ continuous days absent without intimation) = default triggers LOP unless HR marks as approved leave or suspension.; ADVANCE UPON ABSCONDING / SALARY HOLD: If salary on hold due to investigation/disciplinary, employer MUST still deduct PF/ESI/TDS on entitled wages (Suspension Allowance base). Cannot fully withhold without legal violation (₹1k–₹10k fine + imprisonment). Advance outstanding balance carries forward across hold period; recovery continues unless HR freezes installments during hold.; DEATH / EMERGENCY PAYOUT: Salary for days worked (up to date of death) = pro-rata via Salary ÷ 26 × days worked. Gratuity if eligible (payable to nominee/estate). Outstanding advances = deducted from FnF settlement. ESI death benefit + PF withdrawal per EPFO timelines. Default: FnF process same as normal exit (2-day wage rule applies). Override: HR expedites payout within 1 day for hardship (documents justification). Nominee designation in Policy/Worker record determines FnF beneficiary.; REJOINING AFTER EXIT: New employment record created. Continuous service clock RESETS (no credit for prior service for gratuity unless formal rehire contract states continuous service). Advance balances from prior employment = separate; only current employment advances recover. Default: fresh start. Override: HR can designate 'continuous service' in contract to preserve gratuity seniority (rare, requires board approval).; RETRO CORRECTIONS AFTER PAID PAYSLIP: If error discovered after payroll paid (e.g., LOP should have been 2 days, not 3): [Reversal Path] Create negative payslip line in SAME MONTH (if within cutoff) OR next month's payslip as 'Correction – Prior Month' line. Recalculate PF/ESI/PT for affected month (if within same FY) or create Section 89(1) relief if spans fiscal years (employee claims Form 10E for retro tax relief). Default: Correction in next month. Override: HR can issue supplementary payslip in current month if cutoff not passed. Reconciliation Register tracks all reversals; no overpayment written-off without executive sign-off.; PAYMENT METHOD & RECONCILIATION (NO PAYMENT GATEWAY): Manual payment entry only: [Method] NEFT/RTGS/Check/Cash, [Reference] bank txn ID, [Date] actual payment date, [Status] PAID. Reconciliation: Bankfile.csv matches Payslip.NetPay ± tolerance (₹5 allowable rounding diff). On mismatch: create ManualAdjustment line, audit log entry, escalate to Finance. No auto-collection/recurring mandate; HR manually verifies bank account validity per worker record.; STATE VARIATIONS: Shops & Establishment Act varies by state (Tamil Nadu = half-day weekly leave, others = full day). Professional Tax thresholds differ (Mumbai ₹750–₹100k, Bangalore ₹0–₹15k, etc.). EPF/ESI applicability by state payroll rules. Default system logic: HR inputs Worker.State; Policy table pre-configured per state with PT brackets, leave rules, holiday calendar. Override: HR overrides state compliance if employee works in multi-state (applies primary work-state rule).; POLICY TABLE (FROZEN SNAPSHOT): Company-wide defaults: Suspension Allowance %, LOP divisor (26 or 30), Leave Encashment cap, Advance Installment max, Gratuity eligibility (5yr or contractual exception), Pro-rata method, PT slabs per state, Public Holiday compensation (2× or 1×+day), Weekly Off day designation (Sunday default). Policy.EffectiveFrom tracks versioning; payroll always uses frozen Policy version from month of Payrun, never live config. Override per Payslip = creates ManualAdjustment line + audit reason.

Reconciliation register: all arrears + reversals logged + audit reasons.; Leave Accrual & Encashment Pro-Rata: Earned leave accrues daily (policy: e.g., 1.25 days/month); on exit or suspension, pro-rata earned = total accrued × days-worked ÷ calendar-days. **Pitfalls:** Pitfall: Salary revision arrears calculated only for days AFTER revision, ignoring effective-date retroactivity. FIX: System accepts Revision.EffectiveDate field; if effective-date is past (e.g., revised on day 28 but effective day 1), recalculate entire month with new salary + arrears for prior days. In India, working-day divisor is legal standard; calendar-day overcharges LOP. FIX: Default divisor = 26; HR must opt-in for ÷30 with documented contract clause. System warns if override applied.; Pitfall: PF contributions recalculated for salary revision month but NOT for arrears-affected prior months. If arrears recalc pushes prior-month gross > ₹15k ceiling, prior-month PF wrongly over-charged. FIX: Arrears engine recalculates PF/ESI/PT for each affected prior month (if same FY); cross-FY → Section 89(1) relief flagged.; Pitfall: Full-and-final settlement delayed beyond 2 days due to advance recovery processing. FIX: 2-day clock starts from exit-date; wages (salary, leave encash, bonus) must be APPROVED & queued for payment within 2 days. Gratuity = separate 30-day clock. ## india-compliance-law

Indian statutory/legal framework for blue-collar monthly wages in cleaning/facility services: Payment of Wages Act 1936 + Code on Wages 2019 + Minimum Wages Act 1948 + Contract Labour Act 1970 + Gratuity/Bonus Acts. Core rules: (1) max 50% total deductions; (2) fines max 3% per month + 90-day recovery window; (3) wage payment by 7th of next month (all establishments post-Code 2019); (4) 26-day base for minimum-wage compliance and pro-rata LOP calculations; (5) mandatory itemized payslips with 15+ fields; (6) statutory registers (wage register, fine register, PF/ESI records); (7) PF mandatory at 20+ employees, ESI at 10+ earning ≤₹21,000/mo; (8) state-specific minimum wages (Area A/B/C, e.g., cleaning: ₹827/₹693/₹556/day Apr 2026); (9) LOP legally permissible but must maintain statutory minimum; (10) contract labour = principal employer liable for wage shortfall; (11) gratuity 17 days/year service at 5yrs+ (1yr pro-rata post-2019); (12) bonus 8.33%-20% capped at ₹7,000/mo salary ceiling. Non-compliance: fines ₹7,500+/instance, 1-6mo imprisonment, interest 12-18%, adverse inspection inference, worker wage-recovery claims via Labour Commissioner.

**Rules:** PAYMENT TIMELINE: All establishments pay monthly wages within 7 days of month-end (Code on Wages 2019, Section 17). On termination, pay within 2 working days. Late payment: ₹7,500+ fine per instance + 12-18% annual interest + imprisonment risk (1 month first, 6 months repeat).; DEDUCTION CAP: Total deductions in any wage period ≤50% gross wages (Code on Wages 2019). Fines separately capped ≤3% of monthly wages (Payment of Wages Act Section 8). Only authorized deductions under Section 7(2) permitted (PF contribution with written consent, loan recovery, etc.); otherwise invalid.; FINE RECOVERY: Fines max ₹3% wages/month, recoverable only within 90 days of the delinquent act/omission. Show-cause hearing mandatory. No recovery by installments. All fines recorded in fine register, realizations applied only for employee-approved beneficial purposes. Not applicable to workers <15 years.; LOP CALCULATION: Monthly salary ÷ 26 days (statutory base) × absent days for minimum-wage compliance. Companies may use 30-day calendar or 22 working-days method consistently. LOP legal but must not reduce pay below applicable state minimum wage. Impacts PF (12% of reduced basic) and ESI (if gross ≤₹21,000) proportionally.; MINIMUM WAGE (26-DAY BASIS): Central rates for cleaning (effective Apr 2026): Area A ₹827/day (₹21,502/mo), Area B ₹693/day (₹18,018/mo), Area C ₹556/day (₹14,456/mo). State minima vary; employer must apply higher of central/state. Binding on all workers regardless of employment type (permanent, contract, daily wage). Check state labour dept for current rates.; WAGE REGISTER & RECORDS: Maintain wages register showing employee ID, pay period, earnings, deductions, receipts, LOP days, PF/ESI contributions. Records preserved 3 years minimum. Available for inspection on demand. Missing/incomplete register = adverse inference in wage disputes + penalties up to ₹50,000 for non-maintenance.; MANDATORY PAYSLIP FIELDS: (1) Company name/address/logo, (2) Employee name/ID/PAN/UAN/ESIC IP, (3) Pay period & date, (4) Basic Salary (labeled as 'wages'), (5) DA/VDA, (6) HRA, (7-15) Allowances itemized (conveyance, meal, phone, special, other), (16) Overtime, (17) Gross salary, (18-24) Deductions: PF employee, ESI employee, PT, TDS, loan recovery, LOP, total deductions, (25) Employer contributions: EPF, ESI, gratuity (for transparency), (26) Net pay. Each component shown separately. Digital delivery (email/portal) acceptable. No summary-line-only format permitted.; STATUTORY CONTRIBUTION RATES (2026): PF: 12% employee + 12% employer (3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS + 0.50% EDLI) of basic+DA (must be ≥50% gross). ESI: 0.75% employee + 3.25% employer on gross for wages ≤₹21,000/mo. ESI threshold: 10+ employees in establishment. PF threshold: 20+ employees (once-covered-always-covered rule applies). TDS: As per Income Tax Act 2025; Form 130 (replaces Form 16) annual certificate. Payslip figures MUST match statutory filings (ECR, ESIC challan, Form 24Q).; REGISTRATION DEADLINES: PF registration required within 30 days of reaching 20 employees; non-registration from day 31 = violation. ESI registration within 15 days of reaching 10-employee threshold with ESI-eligible wages. Delay penalties interest + prosecution.; GRATUITY (Payment of Gratuity Act 1972): Eligible after 5 continuous years service (no minimum for death/disablement). Rate: 17 days wages × completed years (based on last-drawn salary). Post-2019 Labour Code: Contract/fixed-term workers eligible for pro-rata gratuity after 1 year. Contractor liable for contract labour gratuity, not principal employer (unless law changes). Small establishments (10+ workers) covered.; BONUS (Payment of Bonus Act 1965): Eligible if wage ≤₹21,000/mo, worked 30+ calendar days/year. Minimum: 8.33%, Maximum: 20% of salary. Calculation ceiling: ₹7,000/mo (if actual salary ≥₹7,000, use ₹7,000; if <₹7,000, use actual). Payable annually after close of accounting year.; CONTRACT LABOUR (Contract Labour Regulation & Abolition Act 1970): Applies when 20+ workers employed on any day of accounting year. Principal employer liable for contractor's wage shortfall, late payment, welfare (canteen, rest rooms, water, first aid, latrines). Wages before 7th mandatory. Penalties up to ₹4.8L + imprisonment. Workers must receive ≥applicable minimum wage.; WAGE SLIP COMPLIANCE: Every employee entitled to itemized wage slip per payment period (Section 50, Code on Wages 2019). Payslip is legal proof of wage payment and critical for employee loan/credit applications. Non-provision creates adverse inference in labour disputes. Missing/incorrect payslip = ₹50,000 penalty + wage recovery claims.; INSPECTION POWERS: Labour Inspector can enter premises at reasonable times without notice, examine all registers/documents, supervise wage payment, seize/copy relevant records, issue compliance orders. Inspector facility affordance mandatory. Refusal to inspect = ₹500 fine. Failure to maintain register = ₹10,000 fine. Non-payment of statutory dues = ₹50,000+ fine.; RECORD RETENTION: Wage registers, fine registers, PF/ESI records, payslips minimum 3 years from last entry. Bank statements for wage payment proof 3-5 years. Missing/destroyed records shift burden to employer in disputes (presumption of non-payment).

**Edge cases:**

- **Worker joins mid-month or exits before month-end** → default: Pro-rata salary = (monthly salary ÷ 26 days) × actual days worked. Must not fall below pro-rata minimum wage for that state. Applicable to all statutory benefits calculation (PF, ESI, gratuity). · override: Manual payslip adjustments with documented reason (joining/exit date, minimum wage floor check). UI must flag if pro-rata <minimum wage and block payment unless HR confirms override with reason. · ui: Join/exit date picker → auto-calculate pro-rata → red flag if <min wage → HR override reason required → payslip preview shows pro-rata breakup (days, daily rate, total)
- **Worker absent without leave (LOP) during month** → default: LOP deduction = (monthly salary ÷ 26 days) × absent days. Do NOT reduce below applicable state minimum wage. Update PF/ESI contributions to reduced gross. Employer can document absence reason (unsanctioned vs medical vs other). · override: HR can waive LOP (convert to paid leave with reason), or manually adjust deduction amount if policy allows. Reason must be documented. Cannot retroactively remove after payslip issued without amendment and worker acknowledgment. · ui: Attendance input → auto-detect absent days → LOP calculator shows deduction & reduced gross → flag if result <min wage → waive/adjust buttons with reason modal → payslip amendment trail if post-issue edit
- **Worker suspended/terminated mid-month** → default: If terminated: pay all dues within 2 working days. Pro-rata salary + any accrued statutory benefits (gratuity if 1yr+ service). If suspended (no work, no pay): LOP applies; suspension period capped per company policy & state rules. · override: HR can mark as 'suspension with pay' (rare, policy-dependent) or 'layoff with severance'. Each requires documented reason. Gratuity waiver only if documented misconduct per act. Termination checklist triggers deduction of pending loan/advance. · ui: Separation event form (suspension/termination/layoff type) → calculate final settlement → show gratuity calc (if ≥1yr) → list recoverable dues → generate final payslip + separation certificate. Flag if pay not processed within 2 days.
- **Multiple allowances (HRA, conveyance, special, meal, phone, etc.) + variable bonus** → default: Each allowance shown separately on payslip. For PF & ESI, only Basic+DA counts (not HRA/allowances unless explicitly 'wages' per contract). Bonus calculated on basic+DA up to ₹7,000 ceiling. Payslip must itemize each, not lump. · override: If employment contract specifies 'HRA is part of wages' for PF, then recalculate PF. If bonus structure differs from statute (e.g., company bonus <8.33%), must be legally justified & documented. Nonstatutory bonus (company discretionary) shown separately. · ui: Allowance builder: Basic + DA input, then add HRA/Conv/Meal/Phone/Special toggles (each with amount). Label which are statutory 'wages' vs voluntary. PF/ESI calculator auto-excludes non-wages. Bonus simulator shows ₹7,000 ceiling effect.
- **Fine imposed on worker for breach/damage** → default: Max fine = 3% of that month's wages. Show-cause hearing mandatory before deduction. Recovery only within 90 days of infraction. Record in fine register with reason, date, show-cause response. Cannot combine with other deductions to exceed 50% total. · override: HR can waive fine (with reason) if show-cause deemed satisfactory. Cannot impose on <15yr workers. Must not recover by installments. If combined with LOP+other deductions, total cap is 50%; fine is deducted last if multi-deduction scenario. · ui: Fine entry: date of act, reason, amount (auto-capped at 3% gross), show-cause date required → blocks deduction if show-cause overdue → displays fine register entry → 90-day countdown to recovery deadline
- **Worker earning just above ESI threshold (e.g., ₹21,500/mo gross)** → default: ESI not applicable (threshold ≤₹21,000). Employer must maintain separate records for above-threshold workers. No ESI deduction or contribution. PF still applies if 20+ employees. · override: If wage voluntarily reduced to ≤₹21,000 (rare), then ESI kicks in. If voluntarily above ₹21,000, ESI exempt. No mid-month threshold crossing; applies to enrolled rate for the month. · ui: Wage input → system auto-checks against ₹21,000 ESI threshold → displays 'ESI: Not applicable' or 'ESI: Applicable'. Annual review flag if wage drifts across threshold.
- **Company has <10 employees (no ESI mandate) but ≥20 for PF mandate** → default: PF mandatory (20+ employees). ESI not mandated yet but may be state-specific. Professional tax (state-specific, if applicable). Company still obligated to maintain statutory registers and provide itemized payslips. · override: Some states have lower ESI thresholds (<10). Check state labour dept. Gratuity still applies (10+ workers). Bonus Act applies if wage ≤₹21,000. · ui: State selector in settings → auto-populate applicable statutes (PF, ESI, PT) and thresholds → compliance checklist filtered by state + company size
- **Workers paid via bank transfer vs cash, with delay** → default: Bank transfer is compliant if received by worker by 7th. Cash payment must be before 7th (employer risk of worker claim if delay). No deduction for bank fees on worker. If late: ₹7,500+ fine, 12-18% interest accrues, worker can file Labour Commissioner complaint + wage recovery. · override: If payment delayed due to bank failure, document proof. Employer still liable; must pay interest. No excuse for late payroll process. Worker remedies: escalate to Labour Commissioner → Labour Court if dispute. · ui: Payslip has 'Payment Method' (cash/transfer) + 'Payment Date' (must ≤7th) with red flag if post-deadline. Integration with bank API for confirmation. Late-pay interest calculator + auto-generate interest slips if overdue.
- **Worker at multiple sites (multi-assignment) with different minimum wages** → default: Worker is assigned to primary site per Membership record. If assigned to multiple sites, salary is single monthly figure per Membership, not site-dependent. Site assignment changes validFrom/Until; salary stays fixed unless contract amendment. Pro-rata for partial-month assignments. · override: If contract specifies site-based pay (rare for blue-collar), then each site contributes proportionally. Otherwise, AXHY worker model = single monthly salary, multiple site locations. Attendance tracked per site but pay is unified. · ui: Worker profile: one baseSalaryPaise (monthly). Assignments table shows validFrom/Until per site + role. Payslip derived from baseSalaryPaise, not site-derived. Attendance summary by site for HR visibility, not wage calc.
- **Contractual worker (contract labour) for 2 years (eligible for gratuity + pro-rata)** → default: Post-2019 Labour Code: pro-rata gratuity after 1 year service. Rate: 17 days wages × completed years. At 2 years: gratuity = 17 days × 2 years' salary (last-drawn). Contractor liable for payment; principal employer liable if contractor defaults. · override: If contract explicitly waives gratuity (rare, legally risky), document with worker signature. Waiver only valid if documented severance/settlement. Misconduct (documented) may reduce/waive; must be defensible. · ui: Worker tenure calculator: service start → gratuity eligibility date (1yr post-2019) → auto-calc gratuity on termination → display breakdown (days × years × daily rate) → separation checklist includes gratuity payment
- **Company receives labour inspection; registers/payslips missing or incomplete** → default: Inspector can seize/copy documents. Employer fined ₹500 for non-cooperation, ₹10,000-50,000 for missing/incomplete records. Burden shifts to employer to prove wage payment (missing payslip = presumption of non-payment). Workers can file wage-recovery claims. · override: No override. Compliance is mandatory. Remediation: reconstruct payslips from bank statements + attendance records + PF/ESI filings (cross-verify), provide to inspector + affected workers with apology memo. · ui: Compliance dashboard: checklists per Act (Wages Act, PF, ESI, Gratuity). Monthly audit flag if payslip missing/unsigned. Bulk payslip generation + archival (cloud + local backup). Pre-inspection reports (register summary, coverage status).
- **Worker claims underpayment or illegal deduction after 1-2 months** → default: Worker files complaint with Labour Commissioner. Payslips (or lack thereof) are primary evidence. If payslip is missing/incorrect, presumption = underpayment. Employer must prove actual payment (bank statement + signed receipt). Wage recovery owed with interest. · override: No override. Dispute resolution via Labour Commissioner → Labour Court if not settled. Recommendation: immaculate payslip trail + bank payment proof + worker acknowledgment (signature/thumbprint) mandatory from day 1. · ui: Payslip issuance: digital + printed copy with worker signature/thumbprint (stamped). Payment tracking: bank transfer receipt + cash payment receipt (worker sign-off). Dispute form: auto-generate worker claim from payslip discrepancy, escalate to HR lead.
- **State minimum wage revision (quarterly or annual); payroll already run** → default: New minimum wage applies prospectively (usually next month or as notified). Employer must recalculate all workers at revised minimum. If LOP or other deductions result in pay <new minimum, reduce LOP/deductions or top-up pay. Backpay if retroactive revision. · override: If revision is retroactive (rare), employer liable for backpay. Document the state notification date + new rate. AXHY system must allow bulk min-wage update with effective date + auto-recalculate affected payslips. · ui: Settings > Minimum Wage Rates > State selector + area (A/B/C) > input new rate + effective date > system marks all workers for recalc review > audit log shows old/new rates + recalc payslips flagged

**Pitfalls:** PAYSLIP LUMPING: Showing all allowances as 'allowance' instead of itemizing (HRA, conveyance, meal, phone, special separate). Fails PF/ESI audit, adverse inspection inference, workers rejected for loans. Remedy: Each field explicit.; LOP BELOW MINIMUM: Reducing monthly gross below state minimum wage due to LOP without documenting waiver/override. Illegal. Triggers wage-recovery claim + back interest. Always validate pro-rata gross ≥min wage before deduction approval.; FINE RECOVERY AFTER 90 DAYS: Attempting to recover a fine beyond 90 days of infraction = invalid deduction. Opens ₹7,500+ fine per instance + 12-18% annual interest liability + worker wage-recovery claim + criminal prosecution risk. Automate payroll by 6th.; MISSING PAYSLIPS: Not issuing payslips to workers. Presumption of non-payment if dispute arises. Gratuity/bonus/separation claim disputes tilted against employer. Unrecoverable reputational damage + legal exposure. Mandate payslip issue within 2 days of payment.; DEDUCTION >50%: Combining LOP + fine + loan recovery such that total >50% gross. Illegal. Payslip rejection logic must enforce; if multi-deduction scenario, prioritize statutory (PT/TDS) then proportional allocation of fine/LOP.; PF RATE ERROR (BASIC <50% GROSS): Paying PF on amount where basic+DA <50% gross (e.g., basic ₹2k, allowance ₹8k in ₹10k total). PF compliance requires basic ≥50%. Recalculate with PF base re-defined or restructure pay component.; ESI THRESHOLD MISAPPLICATION: Waiving ESI for worker earning ₹21,100 (just above cap). Annual threshold check only.; GRATUITY WAIVER WITHOUT DOCUMENTATION: Claim contractor/company not liable for gratuity without contractual proof. Post-2019, pro-rata gratuity (1yr+) is statutory; waiver only valid if documented severance settlement or defended misconduct.; STATE-SPECIFIC MINIMUM WAGE IGNORED: Applying central minimum instead of state minimum. States often higher; must use maximum applicable rate. Annual state rate publication changes; system must auto-update or manual override with notification reference.; FINE WITHOUT SHOW-CAUSE: Deducting a fine without giving worker opportunity to respond. Violates Section 8 of Payment of Wages Act. Fine is invalid; must be refunded. Rebuild with formal show-cause process + worker response record.; WAGE REGISTER DESTRUCTION/LOSS: No record of wages paid after 3 years = employer burden shifts to prove. Missing register = ₹10,000-50,000 fine + adverse inference in disputes. Backup payslips, bank statements, PF/ESI filings serve as secondary evidence but are weaker.; LATE TERMINATION PAYOUTS: Not paying final salary within 2 working days of termination. Violates Code on Wages 2019 Section 12. Interest + wage-recovery claim. Automate exit workflow with payment urgency flag.; BONUS CALCULATION CEILING MISAPPLY: Calculating bonus on full salary if ≤₹7,000, but incorrectly treating amounts >₹7k. Ceiling is ₹7,000 monthly salary (basic+DA), not total wage. Bonus = (salary capped at ₹7k) × 8.33%-20%. Recalculate if owed.; CONTRACT LABOUR MISALLOCATION OF LIABILITY: Principal employer assumes contractor defaults on wages without proving non-payment to Labour Commissioner. If worker files claim, principal is liable from day 1; contractor's solvency irrelevant. Implement audit trail.; PROFESSIONAL TAX OVERSIGHT: Forgetting state-specific PT (ranges ₹0-2k/mo depending on state, bracket). Not deducted = non-compliance. PT must be itemized on payslip, remitted to state labour dept by deadline (varies 15th-30th). Check state labour website.; ATTENDANCE MANIPULATION FOR LOP: Recording fake attendance to justify LOP. Opens fraud charges if discovered. Attendance records subject to inspection; misalignment with LOP deduction = evidence of manipulation. Maintain immaculate attendance trail.; PAYSLIP AMENDMENT WITHOUT TRAIL: Issuing corrected payslips without noting amendment reason or versioning. If worker claims overpayment recovery later, no audit trail = dispute. Version all payslips; amendment always shows original + revised + reason.; INSPECTION NON-COOPERATION: Refusing to provide registers/payslips to labour inspector. ₹500 fine + forced surrender + criminal obstruction charge. Maintain accessible, complete records. Pre-inspection mock audit recommended.; UNDOCUMENTED OVERTIME/BONUS: Paying extra amounts without formal record (cash on side). Not on payslip = no proof. If worker disputes or inspection occurs, no legal protection. All payments must be on payslip with clear label (overtime, special bonus, etc.).

## TRUST-DISPUTES-PROTECTION: Payroll Trust, Wage Theft Prevention, Dispute Resolution, Immutable Proof

Make pay UNDISPUTABLE by combining legal compliance (Payment of Wages Act, Labour Code 2023), transparent documentation (detailed payslips with itemized deductions), immutable audit trails (timestamped payroll records retained 3+ years), and structured grievance resolution. Worker must see salary breakdown BEFORE payment with plain-language reasons for every deduction (LOP, fines, tax, PF, ESI). Company protects itself via signed digital payslips, documented approvals (maker-checker workflow), and complete wage registers. Dispute trigger: "my salary is short" → HR produces payslip + attendance + policy + statutory filings as unified proof. Default: transparent, worker-friendly. Override: documented reversal (LOP waiver, fine cancellation) with approval chain.

**Rules:** PAYSLIP TIMING: Issue ON or BEFORE payday, not after. Section 5 Payment of Wages Act: 7 days for <1000 workers, 10 days for larger (wage period max 1 month).; PAYSLIP MANDATORY COMPONENTS: Name, ID, PAN, UAN, designation, pay period, each earning (basic + DA as 'wages', HRA, allowances), each deduction (PF, ESI, PT, TDS, LOP, loans), gross, total deductions, net pay. Clearly label 'wages' (basic + DA + retaining allowance) vs. excluded (HRA, bonus, commission).; 50% WAGE RULE (Code on Wages 2019): Non-wage allowances cannot exceed 50% of total remuneration. Payslip must show wage definition to survive inspection.; ALLOWED DEDUCTIONS ONLY (Section 7 Payment of Wages Act): PF/ESI employee share, income tax, professional tax, pro-rata absence (LOP), court-ordered, fines (max 3% wages/month, recovered within 60 days), advance recovery, cooperative contributions. NO charges for uniforms, tools, arbitrary penalties. ANY unlisted deduction is illegal.; DEDUCTION CAP: 50% of wages if any part goes to cooperative/insurance; otherwise 75% if mixed. Fines ≤3% wages/period, ≤3 months recovery. Absence (LOP) = salary ÷ total days in month × absent days OR salary ÷ working days × absent days (choose ONE, apply consistently, document policy).; WAGE REGISTER (Mandatory): Employee name, work performed, wages paid, deductions, receipts. Maintain 3 years minimum after last entry (Payment of Wages Act). Digital format permitted. Inspector has access on demand.; DIGITAL PAYSLIPS LEGAL: Information Technology Act recognizes e-records + digital signatures as binding. Email PDF or portal access acceptable IF employee consents (express or implied, written consent best). Employees must be able to access, download, store.; GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL COMMITTEE (20+ workers): Equal employer/worker members (max 10 total), rotating chair, adequate women representation. File within 1 year of cause. GRC decision within 30 days. If unresolved, escalate to conciliation officer within 60 days via trade union.; RECORD RETENTION: 3 years minimum for wage/attendance records (Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, Contract Labour Act, Building & Construction Workers Act). 5 years for ESI. Digital archival must be searchable, exportable, secure.; PROOF OF PAYMENT: Bank statement is primary evidence in dispute. Worker must get UPI/bank transfer or signed receipt. No untraced cash.; LOP DOCUMENTATION: Specify entitled leaves + LOP days taken IN PAYSLIP. Attach to payslip: leave application, approval, attendance proof. Auditors can demand this trail.; DISPUTE EVIDENCE HIERARCHY: (1) Bank statement showing non-credit, (2) Payslip + wage register, (3) Appointment letter + employment contract, (4) Communication logs (email, WhatsApp, chat), (5) Witness statements. Digital evidence (Section 65B Indian Evidence Act) admissible if certified.; PLAIN LANGUAGE REQUIRED: Every deduction must have a visible, understandable reason on payslip (not 'Other Deductions: ₹500' — must say 'LOP 2 days @ ₹250/day' or 'Fine for unauthorized absence: ₹500, Company Policy §3.2'). Worker must NOT have to ask why.; DEFAULT STANCE: When worker disputes pay → assume good faith. Provide full breakdown immediately. Do NOT ask worker to 'check their math'. HR burden is to prove deduction is legal + correctly calculated.; POLICY MUST BE WRITTEN + DISTRIBUTED: Salary structure, LOP calculation method, allowed fines, advance recovery terms. Every worker gets copy at hire. Changes = 30 days notice minimum. Policy archived with payroll records.

**Edge cases:**

- **Worker says 'My salary is ₹2000 short this month, where is it?'** → default: Immediate investigation: pull payslip, wage register, attendance system, leave approvals, statutory filings (PF ECR, ESI challans, TDS returns). If short is from LOP → show attendance gap + policy + leave records. If from fine → show incident report + policy + approval. If system error → reverse in next cycle + backpay + interest. Response within 24 hours. · override: If worker disputes correctness of LOP/fine even with proof → allow reversal if: (a) worker submits formal LOP reversal request + supporting doc, OR (b) manager/HR approves waiver (documented). Override recorded in audit trail with reason (e.g., 'LOP waived for genuine hardship, approved by CEO'). · ui: Payslip shows each component with expandable 'Why?' button → click shows policy clause, calculation, supporting doc. 'Dispute this item' button → triggers formal reversal workflow with auto-email to HR.
- **Worker was absent 1 day, LOP calculated but worker claims manager said absence would NOT be deducted.** → default: Policy governs: if policy says all absences = LOP, enforce it. If policy has 'grace days' or 'manager discretion', then manager's verbal/chat approval counts IF documented. Require written approval (email confirmation counts) for any deviation from stated policy. · override: Manager can waive 1 LOP day per worker per month (documented in approval form with reason: 'illness', 'emergency', 'prior commitment'). Each waiver = audit trail entry. Limit: max 2 waivers/worker/financial year. · ui: Attendance > mark as 'Absent' > auto-triggers LOP calculation preview > Manager reviews > can 'Approve LOP' or 'Waive & Document' (requires reason dropdown + free text). Waiver shows in payslip as 'LOP Waived (Reason: Manager approval)' with manager name + date.
- **TDS deducted ₹1000 but worker says 'I am not liable for income tax, I earn below threshold'.** → default: Verify: (a) Worker's annual income across all employers (get ITR/Form 16 from previous year). If below exemption limit, TDS should NOT be deducted. If wrongly deducted, refund in next cycle + submit reversal to tax authority. (b) If income crosses threshold mid-year, TDS kicks in from that month onwards (legitimate). Explain threshold + provide calculation. · override: Worker can provide Form 12BG (declaration of no tax liability) at start of FY → TDS exemption until updated. Company audits annually. If worker doesn't provide form + income is above limit, TDS mandatory (worker can claim refund via ITR). · ui: Settings > Tax Deduction Method > 'Follow Declared Income Threshold' (default) or 'Manual TDS Rate' (override). Payslip shows: 'TDS ₹1000 (18% of salary ₹55,556; annual income exceeds ₹250k threshold; Form 12BG on file: NO [LINK TO UPLOAD])'. Tool warns if threshold not met + TDS applied.
- **PF contribution changed from 12% to 13% mid-month; worker wants refund of overpayment.** → default: Policy must state: contribution % fixed per FY or fixed per wage period. If change mid-period, it's an error. Reverse to correct rate + refund excess in next cycle + file correction with EPFO + explain to worker. · override: If worker voluntarily elects higher PF (VPF), written consent required. Monthly contribution rate cannot be changed without 30 days notice + worker written acknowledgment. · ui: Payroll Settings > PF Rate > 'FY 2025-26: 12%' (locked until user clicks 'Change', which triggers 30-day notice email to all workers + 1-click opt-out). Payslips for 'transition period' show both rates + explanation. Bulk reversal if needed.
- **Worker transferred to different site; assignment dates changed; attendance system shows gap.** → default: Assignment table (workerId, siteId, validFrom, validUntil) must match attendance punch-in location. If gap exists (e.g., left site A on 2025-05-15, joined site B on 2025-05-20) → mark 2025-05-16 to 2025-05-19 as 'on leave' (if leave balance available) or 'absent' (LOP applies). Do NOT create 'floating' days without explanation. · override: HR can override with 'Paid Leave During Transfer' tag → no LOP, not deducted from leave balance, shows as special line in payslip ('Transfer gap — paid'). Requires manager + HR sign-off. · ui: Assignments tab shows visual timeline per worker. System auto-detects gaps, flags as 'Unresolved Gap: 2025-05-16..05-19 (4 days)'. HR chooses: mark LOP, mark paid leave, or mark other leave type. Choice flows to payroll.
- **Worker disputes fine amount; says ₹500 is excessive for ₹50k salary (1% breach of 3% cap).** → default: If fine ≤3% wages for the period, it is legal per Payment of Wages Act Section 7. Worker can dispute ONLY if: (a) fine is >3%, (b) incident was not proven, OR (c) fine was not imposed within 90 days of incident. For case (a), reduce to 3%. For (b)/(c), cancel fine + return funds. · override: If incident is disputed (e.g., worker says damage was not their fault, or unauthorized absence was justified), GRC or grievance committee reviews. If committee sides with worker, fine cancelled. Otherwise enforced. · ui: Fine record shows: incident date, description, amount, % of wages, 90-day deadline. Worker can click 'Dispute Fine' > triggers formal GRC filing if internal resolution fails. Shows countdown to 90-day expiry.
- **Payslip issued 2025-06-15 (payday), but worker didn't receive email; says 'I didn't know my salary was cut'.** → default: Company must PROVE delivery (email open receipt, portal login, SMS confirmation). If no proof of delivery, payslip is not 'issued' per law. Reissue + resend with read receipt + document. If worker claims non-receipt despite valid delivery, require worker to confirm receipt in writing (email reply, portal acknowledgment). Then issue a 'Payslip Acknowledgment' form signed by worker. · override: If worker habitually doesn't check emails, offer alternate delivery: SMS with PDF link, WhatsApp, or printed copy with in-person signature. Document worker's preferred method. · ui: Payslip Release > auto-sends email + SMS. Tracking dashboard shows: 'Issued 2025-06-15 09:00 | Email Delivered 09:05 | Opened 2025-06-15 14:30 | Printed 0 times'. If not opened within 7 days, auto-escalate to HR with 'Worker Notification Required' flag.
- **Company made a payroll error 2 months ago (over-paid by ₹10k); now wants to recover from next 3 paychecks (₹3.3k each).** → default: Advances can be recovered per Payment of Wages Act Section 7, BUT recovery cannot exceed 50% of wages in any period (unless special circumstance). If overpayment is proven + documented, worker has RIGHT to repay voluntarily. Company CANNOT unilaterally deduct >50% or beyond recovery timeline. Must negotiate with worker, get written agreement, document in payroll + audit trail. · override: If worker agrees to faster repayment (₹5k/month for 2 months), get written consent. If worker doesn't consent, recover ₹3.3k/month (50% cap). Company absorbs loss or pursues via court (does not happen in payroll system). · ui: Payroll Error Log > 'Over-Payment 2025-04 [₹10k] | Status: Disputed | Worker: Agreed to repay | Recovery Plan: ₹3.3k × 3 months starting 2025-07'. Timeline shows installments with worker's signature + date. Payslips display: 'Advance Recovery: ₹3.3k (of ₹10k overpayment from April; ₹6.7k remaining; See Audit Trail)'.
- **Worker files grievance: 'Company didn't pay overtime, but attendence shows 50 hours. Pay policy says ₹100/overtime hour'.** → default: Pull wage register + attendance system + payslip calculation sheet. If attendance shows 50 OT hours but payslip shows 0, it's a payroll miss. Calculate correct OT pay (50 × ₹100 = ₹5000) + backpay immediately + add to next payslip with explanation. File correction with statutory authority if applicable (e.g., ESI/PF if OT pushes earnings above threshold). · override: If policy says 'OT paid only if pre-approved' + no approval exists, company can argue policy breach by worker. BUT: if company KNEW worker was doing OT (attendance records show it), non-approval is constructive approval. Enforce payment + clarify policy going forward. · ui: Grievance Form > 'Overtime Not Paid' > system auto-pulls attendance + policy + payslip calculation. Shows: 'Attendance: 50 OT hours logged | Policy: ₹100/hour | Expected Pay: ₹5000 | Actual Pay: ₹0 | Status: SHORTFALL DETECTED'. Auto-triggers HR review + resolution workflow.
- **Worker on contract labour; assignment ended 2025-05-31; final salary (including balance leaves) due within 2 days per law. Company issues payslip 2025-06-05.** → default: Final settlement must include: (a) salary for days worked, (b) balance leave encashment (if policy allows), (c) accrued bonus (if any), (d) statutory refunds (if PF balance available). ALL must be PAID within 2 working days of termination (Payment of Wages Act). Issuing payslip on day 4 is a breach. Pay immediately on day 2 + issue backdated payslip + pay interest (if applicable per state law). · override: If leaves/gratuity require calculation time (complex case), company can request 3-5 days if: (a) written notice to worker, (b) interim payment of salary portion on time 2, (c) balance within agreed extended date. Document everything. · ui: Termination Checklist > 'Final Settlement Due: 2025-06-02' (2 working days from termination 2025-05-31). Countdown timer. Auto-draft payslip showing leave encashment, bonus, refunds. Compliance flag if issued after deadline. Historical log of delay + reason.

**Pitfalls:** HIDING DEDUCTIONS: Payslip shows ₹50k gross but 'Other Deductions: ₹X' without explanation. Worker doesn't know if it's LOP, fine, advance recovery, or error. They assume theft. AVOID: Always itemize.; ISSUING PAYSLIP AFTER PAYDAY: 'Here's your pay' (issued Friday) but your salary hit bank Wednesday. Worker can't verify against salary in time. If amount is short, they only have hours to dispute. AVOID: Issue on/before payday, always.; NO AUDIT TRAIL FOR MANUAL ADJUSTMENTS: HR manually reduces someone's LOP one month because 'they have seniority', but no documentation. Next worker asks for same waiver, denied. Looks unfair + discriminatory. AVOID: Every override = documented + approved.; CONFLICTING PAYROLL POLICY VERSIONS: Old policy says LOP = ₹100/day, new policy = ₹150/day. Worker paid under new rate, disputes it. Company can't prove which policy was in force. AVOID: Freeze policy on payroll date, version number on payslip.; UNDOCUMENTED FINE: Company imposes ₹2000 fine for 'negligence' but never told worker WHY or WHEN the incident was. Worker claims never happened. No incident log. AVOID: Incident report + written notice to worker within 48 hours of incident + 90-day recovery window logged in audit trail.; LOP WITHOUT ATTENDANCE PROOF: Company deducts LOP but attendance system is broken or not reconciled. Worker says 'I came every day' but payroll record says 5 absences. No time-punch data to verify. AVOID: Reconcile attendance weekly. If data missing, assume present.; UNILATERAL RECOVERY OF OVERPAYMENT: 'We overpaid you ₹10k last month. We're deducting ₹5k from next paycheck without asking.' Worker didn't consent. Section 7 breach (>50% deduction). AVOID: Notify worker + get written agreement for recovery terms.; PAYSLIP NOT ISSUED ON PAYDAY: Law says issue on/before payday. Company issues on day 10 of month. Worker can't cross-check pay, can't dispute quickly. Too late to catch errors. AVOID: Issue same day/day before pay hits bank.; NO MECHANISM TO DISPUTE PAYSLIP: Worker sees ₹2000 missing, doesn't know where to ask. Emails HR, no response. Or only recourse is 'file labour complaint', which takes months. AVOID: 1-click dispute button in payslip, 48-hour HR response SLA, escalate to GRC if needed.; POLICY CHANGES WITHOUT NOTICE: 'We're changing LOP calculation from ₹100 to ₹120/day, effective next month' (notice given on payday). Workers find out in payslip. No chance to object. Compliance + morale hit. AVOID: 30-day notice minimum, documented acknowledgment from each worker.; FINE IMPOSED AFTER 90 DAYS: Incident happened 2025-03-01, fine imposed 2025-07-01. Worker has moved on, forgotten incident. Feels like punishment out of nowhere. AVOID: Incident → notice within 48h → decision within 7 days → fine imposed within 90 days of incident.; MIXING WAGE & NON-WAGE COMPONENTS: Payslip shows 'Salary: ₹30k' but doesn't separate Basic (₹15k) + DA (₹5k) + HRA (₹10k). Statutory filings say Basic+DA only. Confusion in LOP calculations (if LOP calculated on full salary but policy says on Basic+DA). AVOID: Always show Basic, DA (labeled 'wages') vs. HRA/Allowances (labeled 'excluded from wage definition').; WORKER CAN'T ACCESS PAYSLIP: Email sent to old address, portal password reset but no notification, mobile app crashes. Worker paid but can't verify. Files dispute 'I didn't even see my payslip'. AVOID: Dual delivery method, read-receipt confirmation, alternate access (phone, SMS, paper).; STAMPED/SCANNED PAYSLIP NOT SIGNED BY HR: Looks official but no signature, date, or stamp. Questioned in court. AVOID: Digital signature + timestamp (cryptographic cert if possible).; GHOST DEDUCTIONS (REPEATED MISTAKE): System has a bug → deducts ₹500 extra every month for 6 months. Worker doesn't notice immediately. By the time they dispute, company owes ₹3000 + interest. AVOID: Monthly payroll review + spot checks. Any recurring new deduction = flagged for verification.; NO HISTORICAL RECORD: Company uses new payroll software → old payslips (2020-2024) lost. Worker disputes a claim from 2024, no proof available. AVOID: Archive all payslips digitally (searchable, exportable) for 7 years min. Never delete.; GRIEVANCE FILED BUT LOST: Worker files GRC complaint, company acknowledges, but then disappears. No follow-up, no decision, no communication. Worker waits 6 months, gives up. AVOID: GRC tracked in system with SLA (30 days), escalation triggers, communication log.; MINIMUM WAGE BREACH VIA DEDUCTIONS: Gross salary ₹30k but after deductions, take-home = ₹12k (below minimum wage ₹20k). Illegal per Minimum Wages Act. AVOID: System flags if any deduction path drops net below MW threshold, requires override + justification.; CONCURRENT DISPUTES (SAME ISSUE, MULTIPLE WORKERS): 5 workers all dispute same LOP calculation method. HR addresses each separately instead of bulk review. Inconsistent resolutions. AVOID: Tag duplicate grievances, bulk review + single resolution, apply to all.; VAGUE POLICY = VAGUE PAYSLIP: Policy says 'reasonable deductions for damage'. One worker pays ₹100 for broken mop, another pays ₹5000 for damage claim. No clear criteria. Feels arbitrary. AVOID: Policy defines incident types + deduction amounts + approval authority. Payslip shows which rule was applied.

## money-safety-fraud

Preventing financial fraud in manual payroll for small Indian cleaning/facility-staff companies requires layered controls: segregation of duties (maker-checker workflow), ghost-worker detection (biometric attendance + master-data reconciliation), duplicate-payment prevention (UTR reference matching), PII access control (DPDP 2025 compliance, role-based viewing), owner-only salary/bank-detail editing, light maker-checker (not bureaucratic), comprehensive audit trails, anomaly detection (salary spikes, round-number advances, zero-device punches), and monthly bank statement reconciliation. The Payment of Wages Act 1936 + Code on Wages 2019 + Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2025 form the legal foundation; blue-collar contract labour requires extra verification (Aadhaar, attendance registers, minimum-wage validation). Non-compliance carries fines up to ₹50,000 + potential director liability. Ghost workers alone cost 3–8% of monthly payroll; one 2025 MP case uncovered ₹230 crore. UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) is the reconciliation gold standard—systematic UTR capture reduces exceptions 40–60%.

**Rules:** MAKER-CHECKER (mandatory, lightweight): Payroll data entry by Role=Maker, independent validation by Role=Checker, both sign with individual login + timestamp. NO single person runs payroll end-to-end. Approval matrix separate for salary + bank files.; GHOST-WORKER DETECTION: Monthly muster-roll reconciliation (attendance vs. payroll active list). Flag any employee with zero device/biometric punches in 30+ days + active salary line. Compare 'Active Employee Count' HR vs. Payroll—must match.; DUPLICATE-PAYMENT BLOCK: Capture UTR (22-char for NEFT/RTGS, 12-digit for IMPS, UPI reference) on every payment. Store UTR in payroll system. Pre-payment dedup check: vendor ID + invoice/payslip ID + amount + date. Post-payment: reconcile UTR in bank statement to payroll ledger within 7 days. One UTR = one payment, period.; PII ACCESS CONTROL (DPDP 2025 hard requirement): Only Owner/Payroll Manager can VIEW or EDIT salary figures, bank account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN. HR can see employee names only (not salary, not bank account). Every access logged: timestamp, user ID, action (view/edit/download). Encryption AES-256 at rest + in transit. Penalties up to ₹250 crore for breaches.; OWNER-ONLY BANK DETAIL EDIT: Bank account, IFSC, account holder name locked to Owner role. Any change requires explicit Owner approval + audit log. NO payroll admin can change bank details without Owner knowing.; BANK RECONCILIATION (monthly, mandatory): Total Net Pay in payroll register = Total debit in bank statement. Reconcile against Salary Payable GL account. Resolve all exceptions same day. Systematic UTR capture reduces exceptions 40–60%.; AUDIT TRAIL (immutable, comprehensive): Every salary cell edit—timestamp, user ID, old value, new value, reason (if LOP waiver/adjustment). Every payment marked-paid—method (NEFT/IMPS/UPI/cash), reference, date, who marked, owner approval. Retention: 7+ years per Indian law.; ANOMALY DETECTION (automated flags, manual review): Salary spike >15% month-on-month (check for unauthorized raises). Round-number advances (₹5,000, ₹10,000 exact) outside normal policy. Duplicate Aadhaar/PAN. Payment to new account. LOP >50% of basic. System flags → Payroll Manager reviews → Owner approves.; VARIANCE THRESHOLD: Any cost-center or salary-line variance >5% vs. prior month requires documented explanation. Typical exceptions: new hires, attrition, leaves, bonuses.; PAYMENT TIMING (Code on Wages 2019 + Payment of Wages Act 1936): ≤1,000 employees = within 7 days of wage period end. >1,000 = within 10 days. Final settlement on exit = within 2 working days. Late pay fines: up to ₹50,000 + legal action.; APPROVED PAYMENT METHODS ONLY: Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS/UPI), cheque, or cash (exceptional, requires written employee consent + documented exception). NO informal channels.; BLUE-COLLAR CONTRACT LABOUR (20+ workers): Mandatory registers digitally—Muster Roll (Form XVI), Wage Register (Form XVII), Overtime Register, Employment Card (Form XIV). Aadhaar verification per Rule 82. e-Shram card cross-check. Minimum wage by state + trade category, revised every 6–12 months.; STATUTORY DEDUCTION RECONCILIATION (monthly): PF electronic return (challan vs. payroll), TDS 24Q quarterly filing (match payroll liability), Professional Tax by state. Reconcile 7 days post-challan submission.; MASTER-DATA FREEZE PROTOCOL: 5 days pre-payment—no new hires, departures, comp changes. 2 days pre-payment—variance approval sign-off. Post-payment—new data recorded for next cycle.; SEGREGATION OF DUTIES core splits: HR maintains employee master (names, Aadhaar, DoJ), Payroll calculates (salary, LOP, deductions), Finance authorizes bank file, Owner approves fund transfer, Accounting reconciles GL. NO overlap.; FRAUD INVESTIGATION TRIGGER: Any discrepancy in marked-paid vs. bank statement, duplicate payment detected, PII access by unauthorized role, UTR mismatch, salary spike anomaly, or ghost-worker flag → Immediate freeze + investigation log + owner notification.; CASH DISBURSEMENT (exceptional, blue-collar sites): Receipt signed by worker, counter-signed by site supervisor, photo of cash + receipt (face-blurred), stored in audit trail. Online reconciliation within same day.; OWNERSHIP CONTROL FIREWALL: Owner (founder/company principal) ONLY can approve: marked-paid action, bank account changes, salary overrides, LOP waivers, anomaly exceptions. No delegation to payroll admin. Dual-signature for disputes.

**Must-haves:** Maker-checker workflow (mandatory dual-person validation, both sign individually, no single-person payroll runs); Ghost-worker detection (attendance-vs-payroll reconciliation, 30-day zero-punch flag + active salary = immediate investigation); Duplicate-payment blocking (UTR dedup, pre-payment + post-payment reconciliation within 7 days, one UTR = one payment rule); Owner-only bank-detail editing (no payroll admin access to bank account, IFSC, account-holder name; changes require owner approval + audit trail); Role-based PII access control (Owner sees salary+bank+Aadhaar; Payroll Manager sees salary only; HR sees name+status only; every access logged + monthly audit by owner); Monthly bank statement reconciliation (UTR match, net-pay total verification, same-day exception resolution, 40–60% exception reduction via UTR capture); Comprehensive immutable audit trail (every cell edit, user, timestamp, old/new value, reason; marked-paid with method+UTR+approver; 7-year retention per Indian law); Anomaly detection (>15% salary spike, round-number advances, duplicate Aadhaar/PAN, new accounts, LOP >50%, all flagged → manager investigation → owner approval); Payment timing enforcement (7-day deadline ≤1,000 employees, 10-day >1,000, auto-flag overdue, red banner, legal liability risk shown); Aadhaar + biometric verification (every worker must have Aadhaar match + identity doc; quarterly in-person re-verification by site supervisor); Statutory deduction reconciliation (PF challan vs. payroll, TDS 24Q quarterly, Professional Tax by state, reconciled monthly within compliance timeline); Code on Wages 2025 compliance automation (basic pay ≥50% of gross, minimum-wage validation by state+category, auto-block if <minimum, exception log required); Contract labour registers auto-generated (Muster Roll Form XVI, Wage Register Form XVII, Employment Card Form XIV, digital acceptable, 3-year retention); LOP governance (cannot override by payroll admin; owner-only waiver with documented reason; auto-calculated from attendance register); Cash disbursement exception handling (cash receipt photo+face-blurred, worker+supervisor co-signature, scanned audit trail, same-day reconciliation); **Edge cases:**

- **Ghost worker (employee exists in payroll, zero attendance, salary being paid)** → default: Monthly attendance report: flag anyone with zero device/biometric punch in 30+ days + active payroll line. Payroll manager investigates immediately. If no legitimate reason (sabbatical, suspension), remove from payroll + freeze bank account + investigate for collusion. · override: Owner can document sabbatical/unpaid leave in writing, store in audit trail, explicitly mark as exception. Otherwise flagged forever. · ui: Attendance vs. Payroll reconciliation dashboard. Red flag: 'Zero Punch 45 Days + Active Salary'. Click to see employee, attendance, last punch date, salary amount, bank account, payment history. Approve removal or document override.
- **Duplicate payment (same worker, same month, two payslips + two UTRs, both marked-paid to same bank account)** → default: Auto-dedup on payslip creation: if (worker_id, month, year) already exists in Payslip table + marked-paid, block second payslip creation. Force cancel + refund. If UTRs differ, trace both in bank statement; if both posted, initiate bank reversal + worker notification. · override: Owner can mark as 'exceptional payout' (bonus, arrears) IF documented in system with reason. Requires separate payslip type (not duplicate) and owner sign-off. · ui: Duplicate Prevention form: 'Worker X already paid ₹Y on Date Z with UTR A. Create as new? [Bonus/Arrears/Cancel]'. Force reason if not cancel.
- **Payment marked-paid but never left bank (discrepancy between payroll marked-paid + bank statement actual debits)** → default: UTR reconciliation: 48 hours post-payment mark, compare payroll marked-paid UTR list vs. bank statement cleared debits. Any missing UTR = immediate flag. Check if transaction pending (IMPS may take hours), bank failed (retry queue), or fraud (account overdrawn, insufficient funds). Owner notifies worker + re-processes payment. · override: Owner can mark as 'Bank Delay' if pending NEFT batch confirms next day. Document in audit trail. Auto-escalate to bank if >3 days unresolved. · ui: Bank Reconciliation Dashboard. Column A: Payroll UTRs marked-paid. Column B: Bank statement UTRs cleared. Highlight mismatches in red. Action: 'Retry', 'Reverse', 'Investigate', 'Bank Escalate'.
- **Wrong bank account (worker reports payment went to someone else's account; collusion between HR and payroll admin to redirect salary)** → default: Fraud lockdown: Freeze worker account + payroll. Pull full audit trail (who set bank account, when, by which role). Owner must approve any new bank account for that worker. Notify worker + management. File fraud investigation report. Check for pattern: other workers same issue? · override: Owner can approve account change ONLY if worker and owner both sign written consent. New account must be re-verified by worker (SMS, call, in-person ID check). · ui: Bank Account Change form: Worker ID, Old Account, New Account, Verification Method (SMS/Call/ID), Owner Signature, Timestamp. Log every attempt to EDIT bank details (even failed).
- **Salary spike anomaly (LOP waiver approved, then salary jumps 40%, then LOP re-applied—collusion pattern to hide unauthorized raises or advance payments)** → default: System flags: salary change >15% month-on-month. Payroll manager must provide documented reason (raise approved by Owner, new site assignment, bonus). If no doc, suspend payment. Owner reviews, approves or rejects. · override: Owner can override if documented (promotion letter, bonus approval, contract amendment). Reason stored in audit trail + linked to salary approval chain. · ui: Variance Alert: 'Worker X salary Mar ₹10,000 → Apr ₹14,000 (+40%). Reason? [Required field: Raise/Bonus/Site-change/Other]. Attach approval doc. Owner confirm?'
- **Round-number advances (Worker keeps getting ₹5,000 cash advances outside formal process, never recovered, owner doesn't know)** → default: Advances require formal request form (worker + manager approval). Logged in Advance Register. Deducted from next payslip (max 50% of net pay per month). Auto-flag any advance >₹3,000 or cash advance frequency >2x/month. Owner approval before processing. · override: Owner can approve ad-hoc advance for hardship, documented with reason (medical emergency, family event). Repayment plan in writing. Stored in audit trail. · ui: Advance Request form: Worker, Amount, Reason, Manager approval, Owner approval, Repayment start date. Dashboard shows all pending advances + recovery schedule.
- **Owner/HR skimming payroll (marks payroll approved + paid, but diverts some workers' salaries to personal account, covers with attendance manipulation or ghost workers)** → default: Segregation of duties + dual control: Payroll admin cannot mark-paid alone. Owner MUST approve each marked-paid action + sign digitally. Bank statement reconciliation by independent person (not payroll admin, not owner). Any discrepancy triggers automatic escalation + external audit. · override: None. This is the top-priority fraud vector. Architecture must make it impossible for owner alone to both approve payment + hide it from bank reconciliation. · ui: Marked-Paid Approval: Payroll admin prepares list (worker, amount, UTR). Owner views summary on dashboard. Owner signs ('Approve Mark-Paid'). System stores: timestamp, owner signature, method (NEFT/IMPS/UPI/Cash). Bank reconciliation reconciliation shows all UTRs within 24 hours. No one can edit marked-paid history.
- **Fake bank account (payroll admin creates worker with fake Aadhaar/name, salary goes to admin's account, covered by ghost-worker pattern)** → default: Aadhaar + Attendance verification: Every worker must have valid Aadhaar (matches Pan/name in system). Biometric attendance required for payment. If worker has no attendance in a month, salary blocked pending verification. Site supervisor must confirm worker identity in-person at least quarterly. · override: Owner can mark worker as 'Remote/Temporary' if documented contract. But still requires at least Aadhaar + monthly phone verification (call worker to confirm bank account ownership). · ui: Worker Verification Checklist: Aadhaar match, PAN match, Site supervisor sign-off, Last in-person verification date. Red flag if verification >90 days old.
- **LOP manipulation (payroll admin marks worker 'absent' falsely to reduce salary, pockets difference via cash advance, then reverses LOP in next month to hide)** → default: Attendance source of truth: Biometric punch (face/fingerprint) or manual muster roll (site supervisor sig + date). No payroll admin can override attendance. LOP auto-calculated from: (base salary ÷ calendar days) × absent days. Absent days pulled from attendance register only, never from admin override. · override: Owner ONLY can waive LOP (documented written request, e.g., medical cert, system failure proof). Waiver stored as separate line item (LOP Waiver: ₹X), visible in audit trail + payslip. · ui: Payslip shows: Attendance Days, LOP Days, LOP Amount (calculated). LOP Waiver line (if any) + Owner signature. Attendance source linked to register date.
- **Unauthorized salary/bank-detail view by HR or site supervisor (PII breach, worker privacy violated, Aadhaar numbers exposed)** → default: Role-based access control locked: HR_Staff role can view only name + employment status. Payroll_Manager can view salary figures. Only Owner can view bank account + Aadhaar. Every access (even failed) logged: timestamp, user ID, resource accessed, result (allowed/blocked). Monthly access audit by Owner. · override: Owner can grant temporary read-only access to external auditor with 24-hour expiry window, logged + notified to worker. · ui: Access Control Matrix. Role → Readable Fields. Owner dashboard shows 'Last 30 Access Log' with blocked attempts highlighted. Worker can request 'Who Viewed My Data' report.
- **Minimum wage non-compliance (blue-collar worker paid below state minimum, covered by false attendance or LOP inflation to hide shortfall)** → default: Automatic minimum-wage validation: System knows worker state + trade category. Monthly gross pay (including LOP) auto-checked ≥ state minimum wage. If <minimum, block payslip. Payroll manager must either: adjust salary, reduce LOP, or documented exception (suspension, probation—with proof). · override: Owner can approve below-minimum ONLY if: (a) apprenticeship wage (with formal apprenticeship certificate), (b) suspension (with disciplinary doc), or (c) state-approved probation (with offer letter). All documented, audit-trailed, worker notified. · ui: Minimum Wage Compliance Check: Worker, State, Category, Calculated Gross, Minimum Wage, Status (Pass/Fail/Exception). Auto-fail until resolved.
- **Missing payment reference / UTR (payroll admin marks worker paid, no UTR captured, worker later denies receiving, no bank proof—fraud dispute)** → default: UTR mandatory on marked-paid: Payment form requires UTR field (cannot submit blank). UTR format validation by payment system (NEFT/IMPS/UPI). No marked-paid without valid UTR. If payment fails, status remains 'Pending' not 'Marked-Paid'. · override: Cash payment (exceptional): Instead of UTR, capture cash receipt photo (blurred face), worker + supervisor signature, amount, date, timestamp. Photo + receipt scanned into audit trail. · ui: Marked-Paid form: Payment Method dropdown (NEFT/IMPS/UPI/Cash). If digital: UTR required (validation check). If Cash: receipt photo + signature required (upload, verify not blank).
- **Worker claims non-payment / salary delayed >10 days (Code on Wages violation, company penalty risk + worker legal action)** → default: Payment deadline auto-tracked: Wage period ends (e.g., 30th). Deadline = 7 days (≤1,000 employees) or 10 days (>1,000). System flags if marked-paid not done by deadline. Daily notification to Owner starting 2 days before deadline. Post-deadline, status = 'Overdue', red banner. · override: Owner can document delay reason (bank holiday, processing issue, worker account change). But payment must still complete + back-date marked-paid to deadline date for compliance. Worker notified of reason. · ui: Payment Calendar: Wage period, Deadline, Days Remaining. Red for overdue. Payment Batch view: '23 of 25 workers marked-paid. 2 Overdue (see list).' Click to retry/investigate.
- **Contract labour compliance gap (company <20 workers, thinks no register required; pays under table; audited, found no Muster Roll or Wage Register = fine + back wages liability)** → default: All workers (even 1) require documentation: Employment Card (name, Aadhaar, DoJ, designation, wage). Wage Register (date, attendance, wages, deductions, net pay, signature). Muster Roll for attendance. Digital logs acceptable. Retention: 3 years. System auto-generates these from payroll + attendance data. · override: None. Statutory requirement. System must support even 1-worker register generation. · ui: Register Dashboard: Muster Roll (form XVI, downloadable PDF), Wage Register (form XVII, PDF), Employment Card (form XIV, per worker). Auto-generate from payroll + attendance. Download + print for worker/manager signature.

## attendance-integration

Attendance-to-payroll integration is the critical bridge between physical presence (biometric/GPS/visit-based) and financial compensation. In India's blue-collar SaaS context, the system must: (1) enforce a binding cut-off date (typically 25th of month) after which attendance is provisional and locked from further edits before payroll runs; (2) map all hours to one of four states: Present, On-Duty, Leave (paid), or LOP (Loss of Pay); (3) calculate LOP = (Monthly Salary ÷ Working Days in Month) × LOP Days, with full audit trail showing who edited what/when; (4) handle late corrections as off-cycle adjustments only AFTER payroll approval, with documented business justification; (5) prevent fraud via dual-approval on manual edits, geofence+device-binding on biometric/GPS, and monthly audits (aim &lt;5% payroll adjustments); (6) comply with India Labour Code 2026: weekly offs + holidays are paid but LOP-exempt, minimum wage via 26 working days ÷ month, basic pay ≥50% CTC, statutory records (audit log, wage register, muster roll) forever. The non-negotiable: every attendance change is immutable once locked; provisional attendance is visible to HR/workers for dispute before final; off-cycle corrections are zero-trusted (two signatures, reason logged); fraud signals (round-number edits, same supervisor 10%+ manual rate) trigger escalation. Defaults: LOP applies to unauthorized absence; waive only with documented approval + business reason; visit-based = 1 visit = present (default), 0 visits = absent, partial visit = supervisor override required with GPS/timestamp proof.

**Rules:** Attendance cut-off date (typically 25th of month) is HARD—no edits after this date without off-cycle approval and documented reason; all attendance locked into payroll calc as-is.; LOP formula: Monthly Salary ÷ Calendar Working Days in Month (typically 26 after excluding 4 weekly offs) × Number of LOP Days; components affected: EPF, ESI, Gratuity, Bonus.; Every hour/day must have immutable state: Present, On-Duty, Leave (paid, deducted from earned/casual balance), or LOP (unpaid, loss of salary). Both reduce from salary ONLY if used and not carried forward (no cash-out except on exit).; Manual edits to attendance by supervisors must be &lt;10% of total records; dual-approval required (entry person ≠ approval authority); **Edge cases:**

- **Worker transfers to new site mid-month (e.g., 15th of month)** → default: Attendance marked per site; LOP calculated per site assignment; salary remains monthly total (unchanged), split prorated if needed for reporting; Assignment record (validFrom/Until) gates payroll inclusion. · override: HR can manually override assignment date if worker physically transferred earlier/later than system date; requires documented reason (site request, worker preference, force majeure). · ui: Assignment edit modal: old_site from/until dates + new_site from/until dates + override_reason text field; show payroll impact preview (site-prorated salary).
- **Attendance marked AFTER cut-off date (e.g., supervisor on leave, attendance regularized on 27th for absences on 15-25th)** → default: All attendance marked post-cut-off is PROVISIONAL and cannot affect current payroll run; rolls into next month's payroll (worker loss deferred 1 month, no adjustment to current run). · override: If prior month payroll already PAID: mark as off-cycle correction, requires CFO + HR approval, triggers separate payslip + audit log entry; if prior payroll still DRAFT: allow re-entry before approval with documented justification. · ui: Attendance entry screen shows cut-off date banner red; post-cut-off entries flagged yellow 'PROVISIONAL - NEXT MONTH'; lock button to finalize attendance after approval.
- **Worker present for 3 days of a 5-day work week; no prior leave approval (unauthorized absence)** → default: 2 days = automatic LOP (no pay for absent days); worker sees LOP line on payslip with reason 'Unauthorized Absence'; LOP reversal can be requested (e.g., manager approval or policy exception). · override: HR/Manager can approve LOP reversal (mark as 'Casual Leave Used' or 'Paid Exception') with documented reason (emergency, family hardship, manager discretion); adds reverse LOP line with new reason; audit log shows who + when + why. · ui: Payslip shows LOP line: 'LOP Days: 2, Amount: ₹xxx'. LOP reversal button: opens modal with reason dropdown (Casual Leave/Paid Leave/Approved Exception/Manager Discretion) + optional notes; shows impact on net pay.
- **Public holiday falls on a weekend (e.g., 26-Jan on Sunday); worker is on 5-day schedule (Mon-Fri)** → default: Worker gets paid holiday wage automatically (no LOP); if site policy requires work on Sunday, worker gets double wage + compensatory off (employer assigns date in next month or worker chooses). · override: If holiday falls on work day: worker must be paid (cannot apply LOP); if worker not paid, escalate to finance review. · ui: Holiday calendar view: highlight public holidays; mark 'Paid Holiday' if on scheduled work day; if on weekend, show 'Offset Holiday - assign compensatory day' modal.
- **GPS attendance marked for morning shift; supervisor marks evening shift manually hours later (shift work, day split into multiple visits)** → default: Morning visit = present; manual edit for evening = logged with timestamp + supervisor name; both visits combined = 1 day present (no duplicate payment); if only morning data, assume evening absence unless proved otherwise. · override: If evening visit documented externally (client signature, work completion proof, visit photo), supervisor can add with override reason; audit log shows supporting doc reference. · ui: Visit calendar: show morning + evening slots; auto-combine into 'Day Present' if ≥1 visit; manual add button for shift 2 with 'override_reason' + 'document_attachment' fields.
- **Worker marked absent for 10 days; later investigation shows supervisor falsified attendance (buddy punch, kickback scheme)** → default: Fraud case: halt payroll, audit all attendance for this worker + supervisor pair; escalate to compliance/finance; mark attendance as 'DISPUTED' in system (prevents payroll run). · override: Legal/labour board order: freeze dispute, implement court-ordered corrections; adjust payslip retroactively with legal reference ID + separate off-cycle correction run if already paid. · ui: Attendance record: 'Dispute' flag + reason dropdown (fraud investigation / legal hold / compliance review); show all edits by this supervisor for this worker; generate fraud audit report (date, count, amount).
- **Worker entitled to 20 earned leave days/year; used 15 days in Jan-Sep; now in Oct, requests 10 days leave (only 5 remaining balance)** → default: Grant 5 days earned leave (balance); remaining 5 days = LOP (no pay) unless converted to casual/optional leave (if policy allows) or unpaid leave request (approved). · override: Manager can approve 'advance leave' (borrow against next year's balance) or 'paid leave' (exception, deducted from discretionary budget); must log reason + manager signature. · ui: Leave request modal: show earned leave balance, dropdown for leave type (Earned/Casual/Paid Exception), approval flow; if insufficient balance, auto-suggest LOP or advance leave with approver notification.
- **Payroll run is APPROVED (not yet PAID); HR discovers missing 2 days attendance, LOP not calculated** → default: Before payment: pull payroll back to DRAFT, recalculate with missing attendance, re-approve, then pay. Audit log shows 'Payroll recalled for correction: missing attendance days.' · override: If already PAID: create off-cycle correction payslip (negative/positive deduction for LOP owed or overpaid), requires CFO + HR sign-off, separate transaction line item. · ui: Payroll approval screen: show 'Lock for payment' button (final lock), 'Recall to draft' button (if not yet paid), 'Create off-cycle correction' button (if paid).
- **New regulation: state changes minimum wage; mid-month, company must raise salary from ₹500/day to ₹550/day** → default: Effective from regulation date (not retroactive); if month already in payroll: off-cycle adjustment for affected employees (difference prorated from effective date); next month = new salary. · override: Company chooses to apply from start of month (more generous) or regulation date (compliant min); audit log documents policy decision + approval date. · ui: Salary structure update modal: show 'Effective Date' field; preview payroll impact for current + next month; flag 'Requires Off-Cycle Run' if applied mid-month.
- **Worker on maternity leave (approved, paid leave under policy); marked absent in attendance system by mistake** → default: HR corrects: change day status from 'Absent' to 'Maternity Leave' (paid category); LOP automatically cancelled, paid leave drawn from maternity balance; audit log shows correction date + reason. · override: If maternity balance exhausted, remaining days = LOP (company policy may provide exception with approval); document as 'Maternity Leave Extension - Discretionary Approval.' · ui: Attendance correction modal: show current status + dropdown to change to Leave type (Maternity/Paternity/Casual/Earned); auto-deduct from balance; show new payroll impact.
- **Worker leaves company mid-month (resigned 15th); final salary must include pro-rata salary + earned leave encashment** → default: Final settlement payslip: (i) pro-rata salary (15/26 days × monthly salary), (ii) earned leave balance × daily rate, (iii) accrued bonus (if applicable). Must be paid within statutory period (state-specific, typically 7 days). · override: If earned leave carryover policy exists, encase accordingly; unused casual leave = forfeited (default, unless policy allows encashment); document per company policy + labour law. · ui: Exit payslip template: auto-calculate pro-rata + leave encashment + statutory deductions; show final net pay; require CFO approval before mark-as-paid (higher scrutiny).

**Pitfalls:** Treating attendance cut-off as soft suggestion: if cut-off is 25th but edits allowed until 28th, payroll becomes unpredictable; supervisors push data entry to deadline, increasing errors. Hard lock + escalation path (not ambiguity) is mandatory.; Provisional attendance never becomes final: if HR can always reopen closed attendance, workers lose certainty, disputes multiply, off-cycle corrections become routine norm (not exception). Audit trail must be designed-in from day 1 (immutable database, not appendable log file).; No reconciliation between biometric + payroll: if biometric shows 20 days present but payroll shows 18 (gaps unexplained), overpayment risk exists. Monthly reconciliation (attendance system vs. payroll) is mandatory.; LOP reversal without governance: if any supervisor can reverse LOP with a comment, reversals become policy-lite + consistent LOP becomes unenforceable. Reversal requires approval (manager + HR) + documented reason + audit trail.; Payroll approval = payment: if approving payroll is treated as payment authorization, corrections post-payment are treated as off-cycle (creating compliance friction). Approval ≠ payment; separate 'Mark as Paid' step enables recall-to-draft if needed.; Salary component mismatch: if basic pay is &lt;50% of CTC (hidden in allowances), minimum wage rule is violated + EPF calculation is wrong. Design-time validation (basic ≥50%) prevents downstream audit failures.; No grace period for cut-off: if cut-off is hard (no exceptions), legitimate late entries (supervisor delayed, network down) trigger rigid escalation. Allow 1-2 day grace period (automated reminder) before final lock; escalation path for exceptions.; Provisional never corrected: if provisional attendance is marked but supervisor doesn't review before final lock, errors persist. Require explicit supervisor sign-off (checkbox: 'Attendance reviewed and approved') before provisional→final transition.; Worker disputes ignored: if worker marks attendance as wrong on payslip but no mechanism to escalate/reopen, trust erodes. Provide dispute button on payslip → HR review → correction/denial with reason logged.; Monthly settled, then off-cycle surprises: if off-cycle adjustments are frequent in month N for month N-1 (or earlier), payroll process is broken upstream. Track off-cycle root causes; recurring patterns = process failure, not exception.; No visibility into why paid ≠ attendance: if final payslip doesn't show the attendance calculation (days, state, adjustments), worker assumes payroll is black-box. Show drill-down: Attendance → State → LOP/Leave/Present → Salary Impact.

## nonexpert-hr-fearless: Making Payroll Runnable by Non-Expert HRs (Small Cleaning Companies) with India-Specific Compliance

Non-expert HRs must run monthly payroll fearlessly with zero training. Core principle: guardrails + smart defaults + plain-language UX + pre-approve validation + undo capabilities + transparent compliance. For small cleaning companies in India: monthly WORKER-based salary (₹/paise ints) → PayRun (company+month) → Payslip (per worker) → PayslipLine (salary/LOP/deductions), DRAFT→APPROVED→PAID, manual mark-paid (no gateway), state-specific minimum-wage + PF/ESI/TDS rules. MUST handle edge cases: missing salary not set, no bank details, absent days, partial months, new joiners, contract vs salaried. Design patterns from Gusto/Wave/Keka: data validation before processing, anomaly detection (compare to last 3 months), "are you sure" before approve, clear failure messages + undo, health check dashboard ("0 unreviewed payslips, totals ₹X vs last month ₹Y"), first-payroll wizard, missing-data blocks with inline guidance ("set salary before payroll" not just error), role-based approval workflows, transparent statutory deduction breakdown on slip.

**Edge cases:**

Cannot proceed until all workers have salary. · override: HR can manually set salary + click 'Use for this payroll' (marks as temp override, flags for permanent config). · ui: Red warning banner on worker row pre-payroll; modal 'Complete Missing Salary' with quick-set form; tooltip '30–40% of CTC is typical' (data-driven default). After payroll, send email 'Please set permanent salary'.
- **No bank account details provided by worker (critical for Payment of Wages Act compliance)** → default: Block salary disbursement; show 'Worker X: Bank account required' with 'Collect bank details' button. Hold in DRAFT until collected. · override: HR can manually collect + upload (photo of cheque/passbook); payroll stays DRAFT pending verification. · ui: Pre-payroll checklist item 'Bank details: X/Y complete' (progress bar); modal for bulk bank-detail collection (bulk upload CSV or one-by-one). After payroll locked, cannot disburse without bank account—this is hard block.
- **Worker absent >10 days in a month (unusually high LOP, possible compliance issue)** → default: Flag LOP days in payslip preview; show 'Worker X: 12 LOP days (₹Z deducted)' with comparison to last 3 months. Allow proceed with confirm-box 'Confirm 12 LOP days for Worker X'. · override: HR can confirm + add reason (e.g. 'Unpaid leave approved') as note on payslip. · ui: Payslip preview shows LOP in yellow (warning); tooltip '12 days = 55% of salary; last month was 2 days'. Confirm dialog: 'Are you sure? This is unusual.' If >20 days (impossible month), red error 'Max calendar days exceeded'.
- **Partial month payroll (new joiner on 15th, or resignation mid-month)** → default: Calculate salary = (Monthly Salary ÷ Calendar Days) × Days Worked. System auto-computes; HR reviews preview. For new joiner: system asks 'Join date?', auto-calculates pro-rata, shows on slip 'Salary: 16 days ÷ 30 days = ₹X'. - **Leave without pay (LOP) deduction but worker has accrued leave balance** → default: System checks: if worker has earned leave balance, system defaults to deducting from leave balance first (per company policy). - **EPF contribution cap (worker basic ₹16k/month, exceeds ₹15k ceiling)** → default: System caps PF at ₹15k basic (12% = ₹1800), not ₹16k. Gratuity calc counts ₹16k (uncapped). System skips 'compare to last month' health check. Run checklist: salary set ✓, bank details ✓, tax docs ✓, attendance ✓. Flag 'First payroll—review carefully'. · override: HR can upload prior payroll snapshot (CSV) if data exists; system reconciles forward. · ui: First-payroll wizard: 'Is this your first payroll?' → guide through setup (salary, tax, attendance). PF/ESI/TDS uniform national; min-wage checked per state. Prof-tax state-specific. ## corrections-history-reporting

AXHY payroll corrections, history, and reporting must balance founder's fearless-HR goal with India's strict wage protection laws (Payment of Wages Act 1936, Code on Wages 2019). The system must enable 2-day exit settlement, off-cycle arrears/bonus runs, forward-only paid-payroll reversals, payslip re-issuance with audit trails, and the reports accountants actually file (variance, deductions register, payment register, bank advice, statutory returns). Edge cases: negative net pay (deductions > salary), partial advances, rounding accumulation, zero-pay months (prohibited), and one-off corrections mid-cycle. Zoho/Keka solve happy path but require manual re-runs on single-employee fixes. Decisive rules: WORKER-based monthly pay (not site-based), all corrections forward-only after PAID status, paise integers always, defaults for every override, transparent history.

**Rules:** RULE: Correction Window — corrections must be applied BEFORE payslip is marked PAID (DRAFT→APPROVED→PAID states only; no reversals after PAID). Post-paid corrections are NEW off-cycle runs, never edit-then-re-run.; RULE: Off-Cycle Eligibility — triggered by: salary revision (CTC change), arrear settlement (missed increments, delayed approvals), bonus/festival payment, full-final exit settlement (must complete within 2 working days per Code on Wages §17(2)), partial payment (advance repayment), or one-time adjustment (LOP correction, attendance fix).; RULE: Payslip History — every issued payslip (PAID) is immutable, re-downloadable by worker forever. Corrections generate NEW payslip with reason field (e.g. 'LOP Reversal - Attend Fix') + link to predecessor, NOT overwrite. Maintain 3-year audit trail per Payment of Wages Act §9 (record retention).; RULE: Advance Recovery — may be deducted from future salary in instalments over max 12 months (18 months in some states), capped at 1/3 of monthly wage per instalment per Payment of Wages Act §5. No recovery from first incomplete wage-period. Travel advances non-recoverable.; RULE: Deduction Hierarchy — priority order when net pay negative: (1) statutory (PF 12%, ESI 0.75% employee, TDS, professional tax), (2) court orders, (3) verified loan recovery, (4) fines (max 3.13% = 1/32 monthly wage), (5) advance recovery. If total exceeds gross, adjust lower-priority items; never force zero or negative net pay to worker.; RULE: Rounding — all money is PAISE (integer, no decimals). TDS rounds to nearest rupee (50 paise = up), statutory contributions (PF/ESI) per EPFO rules (typically nearest paise with monthly remainder carry-forward). Month-end unrounded remainder (< 1 rupee) accrued to next cycle.; RULE: Minimum Pay Floor — no deductions (except court order, PF/ESI, TDS, professional tax) can reduce net below state minimum wage. Zero-pay months prohibited; if deductions would exceed gross, system must flag and HR must intervene (reduce deduction or split payment across cycles).; RULE: Exit Settlement — full-final must complete within 2 working days of last working day (Code on Wages §17(2)). Includes unpaid salary, LOP reversal, leave encashment, gratuity (5+ years), pending bonuses, EPF final transfer, TDS calc as final payout. Off-cycle payroll + immediate reconciliation non-negotiable.; RULE: Payslip Issuance — mandatory within 5 days of pay-run (statutory in Code on Wages 2019). Format must show: gross, statutory deductions, authorized deductions, net, reason for any deduction > 3% monthly wage. Digital + download link in worker portal + email.; RULE: Negative Net Pay — if advance or deductions exceed gross, system auto-advances the shortfall as 'Negative Pay Advance' (NIL net to worker), recovers in next cycle(s) via deduction-priority logic. HR must approve each instance; flagged as 'requires review'.; RULE: Corrections Audit — every correction (edit payslip line, reverse LOP, apply arrear) must log: corrected-by (user ID), timestamp, before/after values, reason, approval status. Immutable log visible to finance + worker in payslip history.; RULE: Bonus / Festival Payments — common arrear trigger (delayed approvals). If bonus approved late, current month payslip = salary only, bonus issued as off-cycle run in separate payslip with 'Bonus - Delayed Approval' tag.; RULE: Partial Payments — if worker/employer agree to split a month's pay across dates (e.g., advance + balance), each instalment is separate payslip entry, sums to monthly salary. Tracked as advance recovery in deduction log.; RULE: Payroll State — DRAFT (editable, no audit log), APPROVED (locked, frozen for review), PAID (immutable, corrections spawn new off-cycle run). State transition workflow with approval gate + statutory checklist (all workers paid ≥ minimum, all statutory deps matched, all corrected lines approved).

**Must-haves:** Forward-only correction workflow: DRAFT (edit)→APPROVED (locked)→PAID (immutable). Post-PAID corrections spawn NEW off-cycle payslips, never edit-then-re-run.; Immutable payslip history: every issued payslip (PAID status) re-downloadable forever, linked to predecessor if corrected. 3-year retention audit trail.; Off-cycle payroll engine: bonus/arrear/exit-settlement/correction runs independent of monthly cycle, issued as separate dated payslips with reason tags.; 2-day exit settlement auto: full-final wizard (salary-to-date + LOP + gratuity + leave + pending-bonus + deductions) issued within 2 working days of exit-date per Code on Wages §17.; Deduction-priority logic: statutory (PF/ESI/TDS/prof-tax) > court-orders > loan > fines > advance-recovery. System auto-rebalances when negative-net detected; never force zero-pay.; Negative-pay advance handling: auto-advance shortfall (worker net=₹0), log as recoverable-advance, recover in next cycle(s) via priority logic. HR approves each instance.; Advance recovery schedule: worker-level tracking of advance-balance, instalment-cap (1/3 monthly wage per Payment of Wages Act §5), max 12-month span. Recalc on prepay.; Variance analysis & reconciliation: month-over-month gross/net/deductions report (flag ±3%), GL posting (salary-payable ↔ GL account), bank-statement reconciliation, documented explanations.; Payslip re-issuance: worker portal can re-download old payslips (marked 're-downloaded [date]'). Finance can re-issue with correction (links to predecessor, reason-logged).; Deduction audit trail: every deduction line (salary/LOP/recovery/fine/bonus) shows reason-field + applied-by (user) + approval-status + amount-before-after.; Statutory compliance checklist: APPROVED→PAID gate verifies (min-wage floor met, all statutory deps matched to filing deadlines, all corrected-lines approved, exit-settlement 2-day rule met).; Reports for accountant: (a) Monthly salary register (gross/earnings/deductions/net per worker), (b) Deductions register (TDS/PF/ESI/prof-tax per worker, totals), (c) Payment register (date/method/amount/bank-ref), (d) Variance analysis (month-over-month, explained), (e) Bank advice file (NEFT/RTGS/check list), (f) Statutory return summary (PF/ESI/TDS/prof-tax by deadline), all CSV/PDF exportable.; Negative-net warning at APPROVED: system flags if net < 0, forces HR to adjust deductions (reduce fine, defer professional-tax, split payment) or approves negative-pay-advance. Blocks PAID until resolved.; Minimum-wage enforcement: system hard-blocks APPROVED→PAID if any worker net < state-min-wage. HR must resolve (reduce deduction instalment, waive fine, split payment). No workaround.; Payslip delay enforcement: payslips issued within 5 days of pay-run (statutory). System alerts if not issued by day 5; prevents payroll close until issued.

## worker-mobile-transparency

Worker-mobile transparency is the cornerstone of trust in Indian payroll systems for blue-collar cleaning/facility workers. The research identifies five critical implementation areas: (1) Mandatory payslip disclosure requirements under Code on Wages 2019 Section 50 (each component itemized, calculation basis shown, basic+DA+retaining must be ≥50% of CTC, employer contributions visible for transparency); (2) Low-literacy UX design (3D/realistic icons over abstract, culturally-appropriate symbols, paired text+icon, card-based layouts, Hindi/regional language full UI translation not word-for-word, contextual help icons on every screen); (3) Multi-channel notification delivery (WhatsApp primary—workers already use it daily, eliminates app adoption barrier; SMS secondary; in-app tertiary—end-to-end encrypted, opt-in consent required, offer alternatives); (4) Earned-wage/period-to-date visibility (daily accrued balance, advance access optional, "you will be paid ₹X on Y" preview pre-payday, attendance-to-deduction line-of-sight via mobile, LOP waivable/editable with clear reason + date link); (5) Mobile-first grievance (48-72 hour response SLA, escalation path visible, can dispute any payslip line from phone with supporting attendance/policy link, resolution timeline transparent). The 450M blue-collar Indian workforce has 13%+ internet penetration growth; WhatsApp prevalence (~80% adoption) makes it the trust channel. Cleaning workers earn ₹556–₹827/day (Apr 2026, Area C–A); contract-labour risk is employer's, NOT worker's monthly salary risk. Non-negotiables: every deduction must cite the policy + date, voice/icon support for non-readers, real-time attendance feed from marking to payslip, advance-wage access without credit-score impact, zero friction in knowing "why ₹X was deducted."
